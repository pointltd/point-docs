Sales shows what was sold at your :term[point]{id="point"}: each receipt, how much money came in, and who sold it. Use it to check a day, a week or a month without going to the till.

::note
You need the **Cash desk** and the access **Open details** for **Sales**. To see the shifts you also need **Open details** for **Shifts**. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it: see [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Look at the sales

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Sales

Open your point, then **Cash desk**, then **Sales**. You can also press **Open sales** on the home page of the point. Sales of this point are shown, not of the whole business.

### Choose the days

Under **From** and **To**, choose the first and the last day. At first today is shown. You can choose up to 31 days at a time. Days are counted in the time zone of the point, which is written under the page title. If you want one cashier only, choose the name under **Cashier**. Press **Show**.

### Read the totals

- **Receipts**: how many receipts there are.
- **Sold for**: the money from all the receipts.
- **Cash** and **Card**: how much of it was paid in cash and how much by card.
- **Refunds**: the money given back, and how many refunds there were.

### Look at the receipts, the days, the cashiers or the shifts

- **Receipts**: each receipt with its number, time, cashier, payment, total and whether it was refunded. Press a receipt to open it. Long lists are split into pages: use **Previous** and **Next**.
- **By day**: the totals of each day.
- **By cashier**: the totals of each person who sold.
- **Shifts**: each shift of the receipts you see, with the sales, cash, discounts, refunds, the cash that should be in the drawer, the cash that was counted and the difference. Open a shift to see its cash in and out and the lines of its drawer.
::

## Open shifts and the daily cash report

If you have the access **See every cashier's shift**, the shifts that are still open are shown at the top, above the tabs, under **Open shifts**. From there you can undo a cash entry made by mistake or close a shift a cashier left open: see [Manage open shifts in the back office](/en/reports/open-shifts).

If you have the access to the daily cash report, the button **Daily cash report** opens the cash of all the drawers of the day: see [Read the daily cash report](/en/reports/cash-report).


## Open one receipt

Press a receipt in the list. You see what was sold, the discount, how it was paid, the change and any refunds. If you can see costs, you also see what the goods cost and what you earned.

If you have the access **Refund** for **Sales**, press **Refund**, type a reason if you want, and press **Refund**. Everything that is still on the receipt is given back. The cash is paid out of **your own open shift**, so open a shift at the cash desk first: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).

## Sales a till sent later

A Windows till that sold without internet sends its sales when it is back online. Each sale counts on its :term[business day]{id="business-day"}: the day it was sold, in the time zone of the point. If that day was already closed when the sale arrived, the sale counts on the first open day, and the page says so under the title. The receipt shows when it was sold, when Partha received it, the day it is counted on and the till. See [Sell without internet](/en/cashier/sell-without-internet).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Nothing was sold in these days."** Pick other days, or another cashier.
- **"Could not load the sales."** Press **Try again**.
- **"This range has more receipts than the page reads."** The totals are lower than the real ones. Choose fewer days.
- **You do not see Sales in the menu.** You do not have the access **Open details** for **Sales**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **The refund did not work.** Check that you have an open shift, and that the receipt was not already refunded.

::note
Refunds are counted on the day of the sale they belong to, not on the day they were made. The [profit](/en/reports/profit) page counts them on the day they were made.
::
