In the back office you see every :term[shift]{id="shift"} that is still open at a point, with the cash in its drawer. From there you can undo a cash entry made by mistake, and close a shift that a cashier forgot to close.

[]{#who-can-do-what}

## Who can do what

You do all of this from **Open shifts**, so you always need **See every cashier's shift**. **Close** is part of the **Cashier** role; the others are in no role, so the owner of the business gives them person by person. See [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).

| To do this | You need, under **Cash desk** |
| --- | --- |
| See **Open shifts** | **See every cashier's shift**, under **Shifts** |
| Undo a cash entry | Also **Undo**, under **Cash in and out of the drawer** |
| Close your own shift here | Also **Close**, under **Shifts** |
| Close someone else's shift | Also **Close** and **Close someone else's shift**, under **Shifts** |

The owner of the business can do all of it.

## See the open shifts

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Cash desk**, then **Sales**. **Open shifts** is at the top, above the tabs. It is also on the **Cash report** page. For each open shift you see:

- the cashier (**You** for your own shift) and when it was **Opened**;
- **Should be in the drawer**: the cash the drawer should hold now;
- **Cash in and out**: press it to open the shift;
- **Close shift**, if you may close it.

If no shift is open, you see **"No shift is open at this point."** The **Shifts** tab lists the other shifts, so an open shift is not shown twice.

When you open a shift, you see its numbers (**Sales**, **Cash**, **Card and other**, **Discounts**, **Refunds**, **Sales minus refunds**), the list **Cash in and out** with the time, the reason, the note and **Recorded by**, and **Cash in the drawer**: the same lines as on the till. See [How Partha counts the cash in the drawer](/en/cashier/cash-in-and-out#drawer-lines).

A closed shift that someone else closed also says **Closed by** and **Why it was closed for the cashier**.

[]{#undo}

## Undo a cash entry

Undo a cash in, pay out or cash drop that was recorded by mistake, for example a wrong amount. You can undo an entry of any cashier of the point. The Windows till has no **Undo** button yet, so this is where entries from a Windows till are undone.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the shift

In **Open shifts**, press **Cash in and out** next to the shift.

### Press Undo

Press **Undo** next to the entry.

### Confirm

Partha asks **Undo this entry?** Press **Undo**, or **Keep it** to leave it. You see **"Undone. The expected cash is back as it was."**
::

The entry stays in the list, marked **undone**. If the amount was wrong, the cashier records the right amount again at the till. An entry can be undone once, and only while its shift is open. After the close, a mistake shows in the shift's over or short: see [Over and short](/en/cashier/close-shift#over-or-short).

[]{#close-someone-elses-shift}

## Close a cashier's shift

Close a shift that a cashier left open, for example when they went home without closing it. You count the drawer yourself, and write why you close it.

::steps{level="3"}
### Count the drawer

Count the cash that is in the cashier's drawer now.

### Press Close shift

In **Open shifts**, press **Close shift** next to the shift. A form opens with **Cash at the start** and **Should be in the drawer**.

For someone else's shift, a warning says that you are closing their shift, that a Windows till will tell them and ask them to close it there too, and that sales made on it until then show up in **Problem sales**.

### Type the cash you counted

Type it in **Cash counted in the drawer**. Partha shows **Over**, **Short** or **No difference** at once. If the drawer is empty or gone, type 0: the whole amount then shows as short.

### Write why

In **Why you are closing it**, write the reason, for example "the cashier went home without closing the shift". It is required for someone else's shift. For your own shift the field is **Note (optional)**.

### Close the shift

Press **Close shift**. Partha asks **Close Aziz's shift?** and shows the cash you counted. Press **Close shift**, or **Keep it open** to go back. You see **"The shift of Aziz is closed."**

::

::caution
A closed shift cannot be opened again, and its numbers are final.
::

### What the cashier sees

- On a **Windows till**, the cashier sees that the shift is closed on the server. The till may ask them to sign in again with their PIN. They count the drawer, close the shift on the till and open a new one. Sales they made on it before that are kept and show in [Problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales). See [Your shift was closed in the back office](/en/cashier/close-shift#closed-in-back-office).
- When the cashier sells **in the browser**, new sales on that shift are refused, and the cashier opens a new shift.

::note
If a till of the point still has sales it has not sent, the form may say (only to people who can also see stock counts) **"Some sales are not on the server yet, so the expected cash and the over or short may still change."** You can still close the shift. When the sales arrive, Partha adds them to the shift, and its over or short changes.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **You do not see Open shifts.** You do not have the access **See every cashier's shift**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **You do not see Undo or Close shift.** You do not have that access, or the shift is already closed.
- **"Type the cash counted in the drawer."** Type the amount you counted. Type 0 if the drawer is empty.
- **"Type the amount as a number, for example 120.50."** Type only digits and a decimal point.
- **"The cash counted cannot be less than zero."** Type 0 or more.
- **"Write why you are closing this shift for the cashier."** Fill in **Why you are closing it**.
- **"The note is too long: at most 300 characters."** Make the note shorter.
- **"This shift was already closed."** Someone closed it before you, for example the cashier at the till. Nothing more to do.
- **"You may close only your own shift."** You do not have the access **Close someone else's shift**.
- **"Could not close the shift. Check your connection and try again."** or **"Could not undo it. Check your connection and try again."** Check the internet and try again.
- **"Someone already undid this. Look at the list again."** It was undone already. Nothing more to do.
- **"The drawer does not have that much cash. It should hold 20.00."** Undoing a cash in takes that cash out of the drawer, and the drawer does not have it now.
- **"This day is in a closed period, so cash cannot be moved now. Ask the owner."** or **"The books are closed up to 13.11.2026, so cash cannot be moved now. Ask the owner."** The books are closed up to today, so nothing can be undone now.
- **"Could not load the open shifts."** or **"Could not load this shift."** Press **Try again**.
- **"You are not allowed to do this. Ask the owner of the business."** You do not have the access for this.
