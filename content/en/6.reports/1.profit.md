Profit is what you sold for, minus what the goods cost you. Partha works it out for every sale, so you can see how much you earned on a day, in a week or on one product.

::note
You need the **Cash desk** and the access **See costs and profit**. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it: see [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Open your profit

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Profit

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Cash desk**, then **Profit**. The profit of this point is shown, not of the whole business.

### Choose the days

Under **From** and **To**, choose the first and the last day. At first the last 7 days are shown, today included. Days are counted in the time zone of the point. The time zone is written under the page title. Receipts, shifts and the home page show times in the same zone, whatever the time zone of your phone or computer is.

### Press Show

Press **Show**. The numbers appear.

### Read the four boxes

- **Sold for**: the money from the sales, without :term[VAT]{id="vat"}.
- **Goods cost**: what these goods cost you. This is the :term[cost]{id="cost"} that Partha remembered when you received the goods.
- **You earned**: **Sold for** minus **Goods cost**. This is your profit.
- **Share of the price**: the part of the selling price that is your profit, in percent.

:::screenshot{src="reports/profit-1" alt="Profit for the chosen days in four boxes and by day"}
:::

### Look by day or by product

Press **By day** to see each day. Press **By product** to see which products earn the most. The table shows how much was **Sold**, **Sold for**, **Goods cost**, **You earned** and **Share of the price**. On a phone, some columns are hidden. Turn the phone sideways, or use a bigger screen.
::

## When the profit looks too good

If you see **Some goods have no known cost**, part of your sales are goods with no cost. These are goods that came with no cost, or were typed at the till as a **Quick item**. Their profit looks bigger than it is. A star (\*) marks these days and products. To fix it, receive goods with their prices: see [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).

::note
Returns count on the day they were made, not on the day of the sale.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Pick the first and the last day. The last day cannot be before the first."** Choose a **To** date that is the same as or later than **From**.
- **"Nothing was sold in these days."** Pick other days, or sell something at the cash desk.
- **"Could not load the profit."** Press **Try again**.
- **You do not see Profit in the menu.** You do not have the access **See costs and profit**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **The numbers look wrong.** Check the days you chose, and check that your goods have a cost: see the warning above.
