Your profit depends on what the goods you sell cost you. When you buy the same product at different prices, Partha needs a rule to say which price to use for each sale. You choose that rule once. Here is how it works.

## What cost is

The :term[cost]{id="cost"} of a product is what it cost you to get it. Partha remembers it when you receive goods from a supplier: the price you pay for each product, plus the extra costs you add, such as delivery or customs. See [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).

When you sell, Partha takes the cost of the goods sold and subtracts it from what you sold for. The result is your profit: see [See your profit](/en/reports/profit).

## The two methods

You can choose **Average (simpler)** or **FIFO**.

- **Average (simpler):** every item on the shelf is counted at the average price of all deliveries.
- **FIFO:** goods are sold at the price of the oldest delivery that is still on the shelf. The earliest deliveries are used first.

### An example

You receive 10 pieces at 10.00 each, then 30 pieces at 12.00 each. Then you sell 5 pieces at 15.00 each, so you sold for 75.00.

| | Average | FIFO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per piece used | 11.50 | 10.00 |
| Cost of the 5 pieces | 57.50 | 50.00 |
| Your profit | 17.50 | 25.00 |

With the average method, the 40 pieces cost 460.00 in all, so each piece counts as 11.50. With FIFO, the first 5 pieces sold come from the first delivery, at 10.00 each.

When all the goods are sold, both methods give the same total cost. They only decide which sale gets which cost.

::tip
If you are not sure, choose **Average (simpler)**. It is easier to follow when prices change often.
::

::note
For accountants: both methods are allowed by IAS 2. The method you choose applies to all your goods.
::

## Choose the method

You choose the method when you create your business, in **Cost calculation method**. You can also choose or change it later in the settings, as long as you have no stock movements.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Business settings

Open your business, then **Business settings**.

### Find the cost method

Find **Cost calculation method**. It says "How we work out what the goods you sell cost you. It decides your profit."

### Choose and save

Choose **Average (simpler)** or **FIFO — the earliest deliveries are used first**, and press **Save**.
::

::caution
Choose before you receive your first goods. After the first stock movement the method cannot be changed. The app says "You already have stock movements, so this can no longer be changed: all your goods must be counted the same way."
::

The choice applies to all your points. If you cannot change it, ask the owner of the business.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **The choice is grey and cannot be changed.** You already have stock movements, so the method is locked.
- **You cannot find the setting.** Only people with access to the business can see it. Ask the owner.
- **Your profit shows a warning about goods with no known cost.** The goods came with no cost, or were typed at the till. Receive goods with their prices: see [See your profit](/en/reports/profit).
