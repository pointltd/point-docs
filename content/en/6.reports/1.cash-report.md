The daily cash report puts the drawers of all the shifts of one day together. You see what should be in the drawers, what the cashiers counted, and whether the day is over or short. Use it at the end of the day, or to check a day later.

::note
You need the access **See** for **Daily cash report**. It is not part of any role: the owner of the business gives it person by person. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions#cash-desk).
::

## Open the report

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Cash report

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Cash desk**, then **Cash report** in the menu. You can also press **Daily cash report** on the **Sales** page.

### Choose the day

Under **Day**, choose the day and press **Show**. From the menu, today is shown at first. From **Sales**, the last day you chose there is shown. **Back to sales** takes you back.

::note
A :term[shift]{id="shift"} belongs to the day it was opened on, in the time zone of the point, with everything in it. A shift that runs past midnight is not split: sales and refunds after midnight count on the day the shift was opened. The **Sales** page counts each sale on its own day, so the two pages can differ for a late shift.
::

## Read the numbers

At the top there are five cards:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Should be in the drawers** | The cash that should be in the drawers of all the shifts of the day, open ones included. |
| **Counted** | The cash the cashiers counted when they closed their shifts. |
| **Over or short** | **Counted** minus what should be in the drawers of the closed shifts: **Over**, **Short** or **No difference**. See [Over and short](/en/cashier/close-shift#over-or-short). |
| **Shifts still open** | How many shifts of the day are not closed yet. |
| **Closed without a count** | Shifts the till closed by itself, with no count typed in, for example when two shifts overlapped. They are not in **Counted** or **Over or short**. |

If a shift is still open, the page says **"Counted and over or short are for closed shifts only. Close the open shifts to see the whole day."** Ask the cashier to close it, or close it yourself: see [Close a cashier's shift](/en/reports/open-shifts#close-someone-elses-shift).

### Cash in the drawers

The lines of all the drawers added up: **Cash at the start**, **Cash sales**, **Cash refunds**, the cash put in and taken out (added up for each reason, for example **Pay out: Small expense for the shop**), **Should be in the drawer**, and once a shift is closed, **Counted** and **Over**, **Short** or **No difference**. A reason whose entries were all undone has no line.

### Card and other payments

The money that was not paid in cash, for each way of paying under **Paid by**: **Card**, **Bank transfer**, **Mobile wallet**, **Cheque** or **Other**, with **Sales**, **Refunds** and **Net**. This money is not in the drawer, so it is not in the cash lines.

**Refunds of mixed payments** is money given back for a sale paid in several ways other than cash. The till does not record which way that money went back, so it has its own line.

### Shifts of the day

One row per shift: **Cashier**, **Opened**, **Closed** (or **Still open**), **At the start**, **Should be there**, **Counted** (**Not counted** if it was closed without a count), **Over or short** and **Closed by**, filled in when someone other than the cashier closed it.

## Open shifts on this page

If you also have the access **See every cashier's shift**, the page shows **Open shifts** at the top, with the cash in and out of each and a **Close shift** button. See [Manage open shifts in the back office](/en/reports/open-shifts).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"No shift was opened on 12 November 2026"** No shift was opened on that day. Pick another day. A shift opened the evening before counts on that earlier day.
- **"Could not load the cash report."** Press **Try again**.
- **You do not see Cash report in the menu.** You do not have the access **See** for **Daily cash report**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **Over or short looks wrong.** Open the shift on the **Sales** page, under **Shifts**, and look at its cash in and out. A mistake made in a closed shift stays in its over or short: see [Over and short](/en/cashier/close-shift#over-or-short).
