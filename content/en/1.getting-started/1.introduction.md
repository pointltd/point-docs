Partha is an app that helps you run a shop or a small business. It keeps track of your goods, works as your till, and shows you how much you earn. You open it in a web browser, on a phone or a computer.

## Who it is for

- **Owners** who want to know what they have in stock and how much money they make.
- **Cashiers** who sell to customers all day and need a simple till.
- **Stock keepers** who receive deliveries and look after the goods.

You do not need to know accounting or computers. Each page in this guide covers one task, step by step.

## The main parts of Partha

The parts follow the order in which most businesses set up. If you are new, go through them from top to bottom.

::steps{level="3"}
### Create your business and add your stores

Start by creating your **business**. Then add each place where you work. Partha calls such a place a **point**: a shop, a warehouse or a cafe (see below).

### Add products and stock

In **Warehouse**, add your **Products** and sort them into **Categories**. When a supplier brings goods, record the delivery, and your stock goes up. You can also write off goods that are damaged, expired or lost, and move goods between :term[points]{id="point"}.

### Set prices

In **Prices**, set what each product sells for. Partha warns you when a price is below what the product cost you.

### Sell at the till

In **Cash desk**, open a shift, add products to a sale and take payment in cash or by card. You can print a receipt. If a customer brings goods back, you can refund the receipt. At the end of the day, close the shift.

### See your money

The home screen of each :term[point]{id="point"} shows today's sales and what your stock is worth. In **Cash desk**, **Profit** shows how much you earned, by day or by product.

### Invite your team

In **Team**, invite your colleagues and choose what each person can do. A cashier can sell but does not need to see your costs.
::

## What is a "point"?

A **point** is one place where you work. It can be a shop, a warehouse or a cafe. Many people say "store", and that is how we think of it too, but the app uses the word **point**, so you will see it in the menu.

Each :term[point]{id="point"} has its own stock, its own cash desk and its own sales. If you have two shops, you add two :term[points]{id="point"}. One business can have many :term[points]{id="point"}, and you can give a colleague access to only some of them.

::note
If you have only one shop, you add one :term[point]{id="point"}. That is all you need.
::
