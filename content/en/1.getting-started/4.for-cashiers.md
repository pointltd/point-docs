Follow these pages in order for your day at the cash desk.

Read the pages in this order:

1. [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift): Count the cash in the drawer and open a shift, so you can start selling.
2. [Make a sale](/en/cashier/make-sale): Add products to a sale at the cash desk, change the amounts and give a discount, then go to payment.
3. [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment): Take cash or a card payment, see the change to give back and finish the sale.
4. [Receipts](/en/cashier/receipts): Print a receipt, and find today's receipts at the cash desk.
5. [Refund a sale](/en/cashier/refund): Return the money for a whole receipt, and put the products back in stock.
6. [Close a shift](/en/cashier/close-shift): Count the cash at the end of the day, see if anything is missing and close the shift.
