Follow these steps in order and you will have your business in Partha, your goods in stock, a price on each product and your first sale done. Each step is short and links to a full guide when you want more detail.

::steps{level="3"}
### Create your account

Open Partha and press **Get started**. We send you an email: open the link in it to confirm your address. See [Your profile and sign-in](/en/business/your-profile).

### Create your business

Press **Create a business** and fill in:

- **Name**: the name of your company, shop or warehouse.
- **Country**: choose carefully, because it cannot be changed later.
- **Time zone**: so dates and times show correctly.

### Add your first point

A business needs at least one :term[point]{id="point"}: the place where you work. Press **Add a point** and type its name, for example "Main shop" or "Central warehouse".

### Add your products

Open **Warehouse**, then **Products**, and press **New product**. Type the **Name**, choose the **Unit** (piece, kg, liter or meter) and, if you have it, the **Barcode** and the **Category**.

::note
A new product has no stock yet. You add stock in the next step.
::

### Receive your first delivery

On the home screen of your :term[point]{id="point"}, press **Receive delivery**. Add each product, how many you received and what each one cost you. The delivery stays a draft, and your stock does not change, until you press **Add to stock**.

### Set a price

Open **Prices** and press **Add a price**. Choose the product and type what you sell it for. Partha tells you if the price is below what the product cost you.

### Make your first sale

Press **Open cash desk**. Before the first sale you open a shift: count the cash in the drawer, type it in and press **Open shift**. Then tap the products the customer is buying and press **Pay**. Choose **Cash** or **Card** and press **Finish sale**.

### See your profit

In **Cash desk**, open **Profit**. Choose the first and last day and press **Show** to see how much you earned.
::

::note
If a point only stores goods and does not sell, you can skip the last two steps.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **The till asks you to type a price.** The product has no price yet. Set one (step 6) and it fills in by itself.
- **The till says there is not enough stock.** Nothing was sold. Receive the delivery first (step 5).
- **You cannot sell at all.** A sale needs an open shift. Open one first (step 7).
- **You made a mistake in a delivery.** While it is a draft, change it. After you press **Add to stock** it cannot be edited, but you can cancel it and the goods leave your stock again.

## Next steps

Once your business is set up, invite your team: in **Team**, press **Invite a colleague** and choose what each person can do (see [Invite a colleague](/en/team/invite-staff)).
