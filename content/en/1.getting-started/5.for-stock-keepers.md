Follow these pages in order to receive, move and check goods.

Read the pages in this order:

1. [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items): Add the products you sell or store, so you can receive them, price them and sell them.
2. [Add a supplier](/en/warehouse/suppliers): Keep a list of the companies and people you buy goods from, so you can choose them when you receive a delivery.
3. [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods): Record the goods a supplier delivered so your stock goes up and Partha remembers what each item cost you.
4. [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods): Send goods from one point to another, and accept them when they arrive.
5. [Take goods out of stock](/en/warehouse/write-off): Write off goods that are damaged, expired, lost or used up, so your stock stays correct.
6. [Check your stock](/en/warehouse/check-stock): See what you have at a point, what is running low, and what your stock is worth.
7. [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count): Count what is really on your shelves, compare it with Partha and correct your stock.
