Follow these pages in order to set up your business and see your profit.

Read the pages in this order:

1. [Set up your business](/en/getting-started/set-up-your-business): Go from nothing to your first sale in about 15 minutes, one short step at a time.
2. [Create your business](/en/business/create-business): Create your business in Partha, the company that holds your shops, warehouses, stock and team.
3. [Add a point](/en/business/add-point): Add a shop, a warehouse or a cafe to your business, and switch off what it does not use.
4. [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items): Add the products you sell or store, so you can receive them, price them and sell them.
5. [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices): Tell the cash desk how much each product costs, and see your profit while you type.
6. [Add a person to your team](/en/team/invite-staff): Add a cashier, a stock keeper or a manager and choose what they can do, point by point.
7. [See your profit](/en/reports/profit): See how much you earned over the days you choose, by day or by product.
