A price tells the cash desk how much a product costs. You set prices once for the whole business, and Partha shows you how much you earn on each sale.

::note
You need access to **Business** and **Prices** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it. Add your products first: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-products).
::

## Set a price

Prices belong to the business, not to one :term[point]{id="point"}. You set a price once and choose the points where it applies.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Prices

Open your business, then **Prices**. You see the list of prices you have set.

### Press Add a price

Press **Add a price**. A form opens.

### Choose the product and the price

- **Product** (required): search by name and choose it.
- **Currency** (required): Partha fills in a currency. Change it if you sell in another one.
- **Price** (required): the amount a customer pays, for example 19.99.

### Look at your profit

If you may see costs, a box under the price shows the **Cost per unit** and the **Profit per unit** (the amount you earn on one unit). If you receive goods first, the :term[cost]{id="cost"} is there. Until then the box says "The cost will appear after the first delivery."

To work out a price from the cost, type a **Markup %** (the percentage you add on top of the cost) and press **Set the price**. Partha fills in the price for you. Your profit is counted on the price without tax.

:::screenshot{src="prices/set-prices-1" alt="The Add a price form showing the cost and the profit per unit"}
:::

### Choose when the price starts and ends

**Starts** (required) is the date and the time the price starts. Partha fills in the current time, so leave it for an ordinary price, or change it for a price that starts later. A start in the past is not accepted: "Pick the current time or a later one." **Ends** (optional) is the moment the price stops, for example the end of a sale. For an ordinary price, leave it empty.

Under each field you see the time zone, for example "Time zone: Dushanbe (UTC+5)". The time is the time of the :term[point]{id="point"}, not the time of your phone or computer. If the points you chose are in different time zones, Partha shows the local time for each one before you save. It is still one moment.

### Choose the points

By default the price applies to all your points. To limit it, press **Deselect all** and tick only the points you want. Choose at least one point: a price with no point does not apply anywhere, so Partha does not save it.

### Press Create price

Press **Create price**. You go back to your list of prices, and a message tells you the price was added.
::

[]{#below-cost}

## If the price is below cost

Partha shows **The price is below cost** when the price you type is lower than what the product cost you. You lose money on every sale at that price. Check the price before you save it. Sometimes this is on purpose, for example in a sale. If not, type a higher price.

If it says the price is below cost **at some points**, the cost is higher at those points than at the others. Look at the cost of the product at each point you chose.

## Find products that have no price

Open **Prices**, then **Products without a price**. This list shows the products that have no price valid today. Press **Set a price** next to a product, and the form opens with that product already chosen.

## Prices that have not started

A price that starts in the future can still be changed. To change it, press the pencil icon in the list, change what you need and press **Update Price**. To delete it, press the bin icon. A message appears with **Undo**, so you can bring the price back.

## Statuses

Each price in the list has a status:

- **Upcoming**: the price has not started yet.
- **Active**: the price has started and is in use.
- **Ended**: the price has stopped. It stays in the list as a record.

[]{#change-started-price}

## Change a price that has started

::warning
A price that has started cannot be edited or deleted, even a few minutes after you added it. To correct it, end it now and add the right price.
::

To use a new price from a certain moment, you do not edit the old one. You start a new price, and the old one ends at the same moment.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the price

Open **Prices**. Next to the price that is **Active**, press **Change price from…**.

### Enter the new price and the moment

Type the new amount. In **Starts**, choose the date and time the new price starts. Partha fills in the current time. You see "The current price of 19.99 ends when the new price starts."

### Press Change price

Press **Change price**. The window closes. The list now shows the old price as ending at that moment, and the new price as **Upcoming** or **Active**.
::

## End a price

To stop a price without a new one, use **End price**.

::steps{level="3"}
### Press End price

Next to the **Active** price, press **End price**.

### Choose the moment

In **Ends**, choose when the price stops. Partha fills in the current time. You see, for example, "Tea at 19.99 will stop at 20 Nov 2026, 14:00 (Dushanbe)."

### Confirm

Press **End price**. The price becomes **Ended** at that moment. A product with no price valid after that shows in **Products without a price**.
::

A price that has already ended cannot be changed or ended again. Add a new price instead.

[]{#offline-tills}

## Tills without internet

When you add a price, change it or end it, Partha shows a message about tills. Tills that are connected get the change at once. A till that is offline gets the change when it next connects to the internet, and until then it keeps selling at the old price. Newer tills switch at the exact start time on their own, even without internet. Nothing is lost and you do not have to do anything. See also [Sell without internet](/en/cashier/sell-without-internet).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This field is required."** Fill in **Product**, **Currency**, **Price** and **Starts**.
- **"Choose at least one point."** Tick at least one point in **Applicable points**. A price with no point would not work at the till.
- **"Type an amount, for example 19.99"** Write the price with digits only, for example 19.99.
- **"The end must be after the start."** Choose an **Ends** time that is later than **Starts**.
- **No cost or profit in the box.** Only people who may see costs can see it. The cost also appears only after the first delivery, and only in the currency of that delivery. If the box says the cost is kept in another currency, the profit cannot be shown for this one.
- **The product has no price at the till.** The cashier can type the price for each sale. Set a price here so they do not have to.
