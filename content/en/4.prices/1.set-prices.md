A price tells the cash desk how much a product costs. You set prices once for the whole business, and Partha shows you how much you earn on each sale.

::note
You need access to **Business** and **Prices** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it. Add your products first: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
::

## Set a price

Prices belong to the business, not to one :term[point]{id="point"}. You set a price once and choose the points where it applies.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Prices

Open your business, then **Prices**. You see the list of prices you have set.

### Press Add a price

Press **Add a price**. A form opens.

### Choose the product and the price

- **Product** (required): search by name and choose it.
- **Currency** (required): Partha fills in a currency. Change it if you sell in another one.
- **Price** (required): the amount a customer pays, for example 19.99.

### Look at your profit

If you may see costs, a box under the price shows the **Cost per unit** and the **Profit per unit** (the amount you earn on one unit). If you receive goods first, the :term[cost]{id="cost"} is there. Until then the box says "The cost will appear after the first delivery."

To work out a price from the cost, type a **Markup %** (the percentage you add on top of the cost) and press **Set the price**. Partha fills in the price for you. Your profit is counted on the price without tax.

:::screenshot{src="prices/set-prices-1" alt="The Add a price form showing the cost and the profit per unit"}
:::

### Choose the dates

**Valid from** (required) is the day the price starts. Partha fills in today, so leave it for an ordinary price, or change it for a price that starts later. The day starts at midnight in the time zone of the shop, so a price that starts today applies from 00:00 shop time. **Valid to** (optional) is the last day of the price, for example the end of a sale. For an ordinary price, leave it empty.

### Choose the points

By default the price applies to all your points. To limit it, press **Deselect all** and tick only the points you want. Choose at least one point: a price with no point does not apply anywhere, so Partha does not save it.

### Press Create price

Press **Create price**. You go back to your list of prices, and a message tells you the price was added.
::

::caution
If the box shows "The price is below cost", you sell this product at a loss. Check the price before you save it.
::

## Find products that have no price

Open **Prices**, then **Products without a price**. This list shows the products that have no price valid today. Press **Set a price** next to a product, and the form opens with that product already chosen.

## Change or delete a price

- To change a price, press the pencil icon in the list, change what you need and press **Update Price**.
- To delete a price, press the bin icon. A message appears with **Undo**, so you can bring the price back.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This field is required."** Fill in **Product**, **Currency**, **Price** and **Valid from**.
- **"Choose at least one point."** Tick at least one point in **Applicable points**. A price with no point would not work at the till.
- **"Type an amount, for example 19.99"** Write the price with digits only, for example 19.99.
- **"The end date cannot be before the start date."** Choose a **Valid to** date that is the same as or later than **Valid from**.
- **No cost or profit in the box.** Only people who may see costs can see it. The cost also appears only after the first delivery, and only in the currency of that delivery. If the box says the cost is kept in another currency, the profit cannot be shown for this one.
- **The product has no price at the till.** The cashier can type the price for each sale. Set a price here so they do not have to.
