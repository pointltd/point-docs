Most of Partha works the same in every country. This section covers only what is different for a business in Tajikistan: the currency, VAT and receipts.

::note
These pages describe what the app does today. They are not tax advice. For your own case, ask your accountant or the tax office.
::

## What is set for Tajikistan

- **Currency.** The somoni (TJS) is offered first when you create prices and documents. You can still choose another currency.
- **VAT.** Partha knows the VAT rates of Tajikistan and changes the standard rate by itself on 1 January 2027.
- **Receipts.** Partha prints the VAT and your tax number on the receipt.

You choose the country once, when you create the business: see [Create your business](/en/business/create-business). It cannot be changed later.

## What is not covered yet

Partha does not connect to a cash register approved by the tax authority, and it does not send anything to the tax office. Read the details on the next page.
