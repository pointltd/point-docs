If your business is in Tajikistan, Partha works out :term[VAT]{id="vat"} with the rates of Tajikistan and prints it on your receipts. This page lists what the app does and what it does not do yet.

::warning
These rates were checked on 2 November 2026 against the Tax Code of Tajikistan. Laws change. Before you rely on them, confirm with your accountant or the tax office.
::

## VAT rates

Turn VAT on only if you are registered for it: see [Set up VAT](/en/business/set-up-vat). Then Partha uses these categories:

| Category in the app | Rate | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard rate | 14% until 31 December 2026, then 13% from 1 January 2027 | Most goods |
| Zero rate (exports) | 0% | Goods you export |
| Exempt | No VAT | Goods that the law frees from VAT |

- The **Usual VAT rate** in **Business settings** is used for every product that has no rate of its own. For a new business it is the standard rate.
- To give one product another category, change **VAT** when you add the product: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
- On 1 January 2027 Partha changes the standard rate by itself. A receipt that was already printed keeps the rate it had on its day.
- Shelf prices already include VAT. At the till you do not add it: the receipt shows how much of the total is VAT.

## Your tax number and the receipt

When VAT is on, you type your **Tax number**. Partha prints it on every receipt, together with the VAT. When VAT is off, the receipt has neither. See [Receipts](/en/cashier/receipts).

## When you must register for VAT

The Tax Code says a business registers for VAT when its sales in up to 12 months go above 1,000,000 somoni. Partha does not check this for you and does not warn you. Watch your sales yourself, or ask your accountant.

## What Partha does not do yet

::caution
Partha does not connect to a cash register approved by the tax authority, or to any system of the tax office. A receipt printed from Partha does not replace what the law asks of you. Ask your accountant or the tax office what your business needs, and whether you still need an approved cash register.
::

- **Reduced VAT rates.** The Tax Code has reduced rates for some activities, for example 7% for construction, hotels and catering, and 5% for farm products you grow yourself, education and sanatorium care. Partha does not have these rates yet. If your goods fall under one, the VAT on the receipt may not be right.
- **Other taxes and reports.** Partha works out only VAT. It does not work out other taxes or prepare reports for the tax office.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **The receipt shows no VAT.** VAT is off for your business. Turn on **My business charges VAT** if you are registered for it.
- **The VAT on a receipt is not what you expected.** Check that VAT is on, and the category of the product. Receipts already printed never change.
- **You need a receipt with a number from the tax office.** Partha cannot print it yet. See the warning above.
