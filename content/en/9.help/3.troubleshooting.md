Find your problem below, grouped by what you were doing. Each line gives the short fix and a link to the page with the full answer. The same problems are listed at the end of each page, under **If something went wrong**.

## Account and sign-in

- **The email with the link does not come.** Wait a minute, check your spam folder, then press **Send the link again**. Full answer: [Your profile and sign-in](/en/business/your-profile#troubleshooting).
- **You forgot your password.** Press **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. Full answer: [Reset a forgotten password](/en/business/reset-password).

## Your business and VAT

- **You chose the wrong country.** It cannot be changed. Create a new business with the right country and delete the wrong one. Full answer: [Change your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#troubleshooting).
- **The Tax number is empty and VAT will not save.** The tax number is required when **My business charges VAT** is on. Full answer: [Set up VAT](/en/business/set-up-vat#troubleshooting).
- **You cannot press Save, or a switch is grey.** You do not have access to change the business. Ask the owner. Full answer: [Change your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#troubleshooting).

## Products and stock

- **You received goods, but your stock did not grow.** The delivery is still a draft. Open it and press **Add to stock**. Full answer: [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods#troubleshooting).
- **You made a mistake in a delivery.** Change it while it is a draft. After **Add to stock** you cancel it and record it again. Full answer: [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods#troubleshooting).
- **"Another product already has this barcode."** Each barcode belongs to one product. Find the other product or type a different barcode. Full answer: [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items#troubleshooting).
- **You do not see the move at the receiving point.** Open the list again. Check that the sending point pressed **Send goods**. Full answer: [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods#troubleshooting).
- **The stock number is wrong.** Goods can leave by a write-off or a move. Check those lists. Full answer: [Check your stock](/en/warehouse/check-stock#troubleshooting).

## Stock counts

- **A product is already in another count.** One product can be in only one open count. Finish or cancel the other count. Full answer: [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#troubleshooting).
- **You cannot check or post a count.** You counted in it yourself. Ask another person. Full answer: [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#troubleshooting).
- **Only the owner can post the count.** Its total difference is over your limit. Full answer: [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#large-differences).
- **You cannot post: a product is in a move on the way.** Receive or cancel the move first. Full answer: [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#troubleshooting).
- **You cannot cancel a delivery, a write-off or a move.** A posted stock count already checked those goods. Record a new document instead. Full answer: [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#counted-since).

## Prices

- **The product has no price at the till.** Set a price in **Prices**. Until then, the cashier types the price for each sale. Full answer: [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices#troubleshooting).
- **"Type an amount, for example 19.99".** Write the price with digits only. Full answer: [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices#troubleshooting).

## Cash desk

- **"Open a shift first."** A sale needs an open shift. Press **Open a shift**. Full answer: [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
- **"There is not enough stock… Nothing was sold."** Remove the product from the sale, or receive the goods first. Full answer: [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment#troubleshooting).
- **You are not sure the sale went through.** Press the button again. The same sale is never recorded twice. Full answer: [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment#troubleshooting).
- **The print window does not open.** Check that your browser allows printing and your printer is connected. Full answer: [Receipts](/en/cashier/receipts#troubleshooting).
- **There is no **Refund this receipt** button.** The receipt was already refunded. Full answer: [Refund a sale](/en/cashier/refund#troubleshooting).
- **Cash is missing or extra when you close.** Count again, check the sales and refunds, and write the reason in the note. Full answer: [Close a shift](/en/cashier/close-shift#troubleshooting).

## Reports

- **Your profit looks too good, or the numbers look wrong.** Check the days you chose, and check that your goods have a cost. Full answer: [See your profit](/en/reports/profit#troubleshooting).
- **You cannot change the cost method.** It is locked after the first stock movement. Full answer: [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works#troubleshooting).

## Team and access

- **You do not see a menu item or a button.** You do not have that access. Ask the owner of the business. Full answer: [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
- **"Give access to at least one point."** Choose a role for at least one point. Full answer: [Add a person to your team](/en/team/invite-staff).
