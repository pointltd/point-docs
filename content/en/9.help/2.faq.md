## Your business

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Can I use Partha only for a warehouse, without selling?" icon="i-lucide-warehouse"}
  Yes. A :term[point]{id="point"} can be a warehouse that never sells. Switch off the cash desk at that point: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Can I change the country of my business?" icon="i-lucide-globe"}
  No. The country is chosen when you create the business. If it is wrong, create a new business with the right country and delete the wrong one: see [Change your business settings](/en/business/business-settings).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="What does my business need to turn on VAT?" icon="i-lucide-percent"}
  You need to be registered for :term[VAT]{id="vat"}, and you type your tax number. See [Set up VAT](/en/business/set-up-vat).
  :::
::

## Warehouse

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="I received goods, but my stock did not grow." icon="i-lucide-package-plus"}
  The delivery is still a :term[draft]{id="draft"}. Open it and press **Add to stock**. See [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="I made a mistake in a delivery I already added." icon="i-lucide-undo-2"}
  You cannot edit it, but you can cancel it and record it again. Partha keeps the cancelled one in the list. See [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="How do I get a notification when a product runs low?" icon="i-lucide-bell"}
  Type a **Minimum stock** on the product. See [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items) and [Check your stock](/en/warehouse/check-stock).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="How do I send goods from my warehouse to my shop?" icon="i-lucide-arrow-left-right"}
  Record a move. The shop accepts the goods when they arrive. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
  :::
::

## Prices and profit

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="A product has no price at the till." icon="i-lucide-tag"}
  The cashier can type the price for each sale. To avoid that, set a price: see [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Why does my profit look too good?" icon="i-lucide-coins"}
  Some goods have no known cost, for example goods typed as a **Quick item**. See [See your profit](/en/reports/profit).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Can I change the cost method later?" icon="i-lucide-calculator"}
  Not after your first stock movement. See [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works).
  :::
::

## Cash desk

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Why can I not start selling?" icon="i-lucide-key-round"}
  A sale needs an open shift. See [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Can I take a card payment?" icon="i-lucide-credit-card"}
  The cash desk is not connected to a card terminal. Charge the card on your terminal, then finish the sale. See [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Can I refund only some of the products on a receipt?" icon="i-lucide-receipt"}
  No. You refund the whole receipt, and only once. See [Refund a sale](/en/cashier/refund).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="The cash does not match when I close my shift." icon="i-lucide-lock"}
  Count again, check the sales and refunds, and write the reason in the note. See [Close a shift](/en/cashier/close-shift).
  :::
::

## Your team

::accordion
  :::accordion-item{label="Can a colleague see my costs and profit?" icon="i-lucide-shield-check"}
  Only if you give them the access **See costs and profit**. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
  :::

  :::accordion-item{label="Someone left. How do I stop their access?" icon="i-lucide-user-x"}
  Set **No access** at every point. See [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).
  :::
::
