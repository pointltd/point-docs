Here we write what you can do in Partha and where, one entry for each release, newest first. We write only what you will notice, and we link the page that explains it.

## 6 November 2026: see and cancel invitations

Under **Team** there is a new list, **Waiting for an answer**, with the people you invited by email who have not joined yet. Press **Cancel invitation** if you sent one by mistake. The setup step **Invite a colleague** on the home page is now ticked once you have invited someone. See [Add a person to your team](/en/team/invite-staff).

## 5 November 2026: count your stock

There is a new **Stock counts** page under **Warehouse**. One person counts the goods on the shelves without seeing what Partha expects. Another person checks the difference for each product, asks for a recount if needed, and presses **Update stock**. Then Partha corrects your stock. Give the people who count the new **Stock counter** role, and set the limits for large differences in **Business settings**. See [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count).

## 5 November 2026: see your sales in the back office

There is a new **Sales** page under **Cash desk**. See every receipt of the days you choose, the totals for cash and card, the sales by day and by cashier, and the shifts. If you may refund, you can also refund a receipt from there. See [See your sales](/en/reports/sales).

## 5 November 2026: sign in at the till as the owner

Two new answers for the Windows till. If you own the business, you are already on your **Team**: press **Give a new code** next to your name, then choose your PIN at the till. To let your colleagues sell, give them the **Cashier** role for the point and a code of their own. See [Let people sign in at the till](/en/team/till-sign-in).

## 5 November 2026: the Windows till in your language

The Partha Till app for Windows now has a language button for English, Russian and Tajik, on the code screen, on the **Who is selling?** screen and in the till menu. The installer you download from the **Tills** page now speaks the language you use Partha in, and the app starts in that language. See [Install the Partha Till app on Windows](/en/cashier/install-till-app).

## 4 November 2026: reset a forgotten password

If you forget your password, press **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and we email you a link to choose a new one. See [Reset a forgotten password](/en/business/reset-password).

## 4 November 2026: numbers on receipts and moves

Goods you receive and goods you move now get a number of their own, the way goods taken out already do.

- **Receiving goods:** when you press **Add to stock**, the delivery gets the next number of your point: GR-000001, GR-000002 and so on. Your own number for the delivery stays as you typed it. See [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
- **Moving goods:** when you press **Send goods**, the move gets the next number of the sending point: TR-000001, TR-000002 and so on. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
- **Taking goods out:** the numbers WO-000001, WO-000002 and so on are given when you press **Take out of stock**. See [Take goods out of stock](/en/warehouse/write-off).
- A draft has no number, numbers have no gaps, and a number is never changed. You cannot type a number like WO-000001 or TR-000001 yourself.


## 3 November 2026: what Partha can do today

This is the first entry. It sums up what you can do now, so you know where to start.

**Your business**

- Create your account and your business, and add your shops, warehouses and cafes as :term[points]{id="point"}. See [Create your business](/en/business/create-business) and [Add a point](/en/business/add-point).
- Tell Partha whether you charge :term[VAT]{id="vat"}, and choose how your cost is counted. See [Set up VAT](/en/business/set-up-vat) and [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works).
- Use Partha in English, Russian or Tajik, in light or dark colours. See [Your profile and sign-in](/en/business/your-profile).

**Warehouse and prices**

- Add products, suppliers and categories, receive goods, move them between points and take them out of stock. See [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items) and [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
- Check your :term[stock]{id="stock"} and see which products run low. See [Check your stock](/en/warehouse/check-stock).
- Set a price for each product. See [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices).

**Cash desk**

- Open a :term[shift]{id="shift"}, sell, take payment, print a receipt, refund a whole receipt and close the shift. See [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift) and [Make a sale](/en/cashier/make-sale).
- Use the Partha till app on Windows. See [Install the Partha till app on Windows](/en/cashier/install-till-app).

**Profit and team**

- See your profit for the days you choose. See [See your profit](/en/reports/profit).
- Invite colleagues and choose what each can do. See [Add a person to your team](/en/team/invite-staff) and [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).

**Not ready yet**

- The **Plan** page is empty and the app takes no payments: see [Plan and billing](/en/business/plan-and-billing).
- In Tajikistan, Partha does not connect to a cash register approved by the tax authority: see [Taxes and receipts in Tajikistan](/en/markets/tajikistan/taxes).
- You cannot change your name or password in the app.
