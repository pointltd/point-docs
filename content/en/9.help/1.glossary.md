Short answers to "what does this word mean?". A word with a dotted underline in this guide shows its explanation when you hover over it or tap it.

## Point

A **point** is one place where you work: a shop, a store, a warehouse or a cafe. Each point has its own stock, its own cash desk and its own sales. A business can have many points.

For example, if you own a shop and a warehouse, you add two points, and you can move goods between them. You can also give a colleague access to only one of them.

[]{#vat}

## VAT

**VAT** (value added tax) is a tax that is part of the price of goods. If your business charges VAT, Partha shows it on receipts. Whether you charge VAT, and at what rate, depends on your country and your business.

[]{#draft}

## Draft

A **draft** is a document you have started but not finished, for example a delivery you are still writing down. A draft changes nothing in your stock. When you complete it, it becomes final: it can no longer be edited, only cancelled.

[]{#stock}

## Stock

**Stock** is the goods you have on hand. Partha counts it for each point, and it goes up when you receive goods and down when you sell, write off or move them.

[]{#cost}

## Cost

**Cost** is what a product cost you to get, for example the price you paid the supplier plus delivery. Partha remembers it when you receive goods and uses it to show your profit: the price you sell at, minus the cost.

[]{#shift}

## Shift

A **shift** is your working time at the till. You open it with the cash that is in the drawer and close it after counting the cash again. Partha counts the sales of each shift, and tells you how much cash should be in the drawer at the end.

## Stock count

A **stock count** is when you count the goods that are really at a point and compare them with what Partha has. For example, Partha has 10 bottles of juice, and you find 8 on the shelf. After another person checks the count and updates the stock, Partha corrects it. See [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count).

## Difference

A **difference** is how much a count differs from Partha. If there are fewer goods on the shelf than in Partha, it is a **shortage**. If there are more, it is a **surplus**. For example, Partha has 10 bottles and you count 8: a shortage of 2.

## Blind count

In a **blind count**, the person who counts does not see how many goods Partha expects. They write what they really see, so they cannot copy the number from the screen or hide a shortage. It works only if the counter cannot see the stock anywhere else: give them the **Stock counter** role.

## Recount

A **recount** is counting a product again, by another person, when the first number looks wrong. For example, the counter found 2 bottles missing, and a colleague counts the shelf again. Partha always uses the latest count.
