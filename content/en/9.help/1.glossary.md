Short answers to "what does this word mean?". A word with a dotted underline in this guide shows its explanation when you hover over it or tap it.

## Point

A **point** is one place where you work: a shop, a store, a warehouse or a cafe. Each point has its own stock, its own cash desk and its own sales. A business can have many points.

For example, if you own a shop and a warehouse, you add two points, and you can move goods between them. You can also give a colleague access to only one of them.

[]{#vat}

## VAT

**VAT** (value added tax) is a tax that is part of the price of goods. If your business charges VAT, Partha shows it on receipts. Whether you charge VAT, and at what rate, depends on your country and your business.

[]{#draft}

## Draft

A **draft** is a document you have started but not finished, for example a delivery you are still writing down. A draft changes nothing in your stock. When you complete it, it becomes final: it can no longer be edited, only cancelled.

[]{#stock}

## Stock

**Stock** is the goods you have on hand. Partha counts it for each point, and it goes up when you receive goods and down when you sell, write off or move them.

[]{#cost}

## Cost

**Cost** is what a product cost you to get, for example the price you paid the supplier plus delivery. Partha remembers it when you receive goods and uses it to show your profit: the price you sell at, minus the cost.
