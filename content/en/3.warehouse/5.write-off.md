Sometimes goods are no longer for sale: they are damaged, they have expired, or they got lost. Take them out of your stock in Partha, and write why. Then your stock shows what you really have.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it.
::

## Take goods out

::steps{level="3"}
### Start the write-off

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Warehouse**, then **Goods taken out**. Press **Take out goods**. A new :term[draft]{id="draft"} opens. Your :term[stock]{id="stock"} does not change until you finish it.

### Say why

Under **Why are these goods taken out?**, choose the reason:

- **Damaged**
- **Expired**
- **Lost**
- **Stolen**
- **Used by the business**
- **Given as a sample**
- **Other**

### Fill in the rest of the top

- **Taken from point**: your point. You cannot change it.
- **Date**: the date of the write-off.
- **Document number (optional)** and **Note (optional)**: anything that helps you find it later. If you leave the number empty, Partha gives the write-off the next number (WO-000001, WO-000002 and so on) when you take the goods out. A draft has no number yet, and a number is never changed afterwards. Do not type numbers like WO-000001 or TR-000001 yourself: Partha keeps them for its own numbers.

### Add the products

Scan a barcode or type a name in **Scan or type a product**. For each product, type the **Quantity** to take out.

You do not type a price. Partha takes the cost of the goods from your stock when you finish the document, and shows it on the list and in the document.

### Press Take out of stock

Press **Take out of stock**. Partha asks **Take these goods out of your stock?** Confirm. Your stock goes down by the products on the list, and the document can no longer be edited.

:::
::

## Fix a mistake

- **While it is a draft**, change anything you like, or press **Delete draft**.
- **After you took the goods out**, the document cannot be edited. Open it and press **Cancel this write-off**, and write **Why are you cancelling it?** if you like. The goods come back into your stock. The write-off stays in the list, marked as cancelled.

::caution
Cancelling a write-off cannot be undone. If you need to take the goods out again, record a new write-off.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **You cannot find the product.** Search by name or barcode. If the product is not in your list yet, add it first: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
- **You took out the wrong goods.** Cancel the write-off and record a new one.
- **You do not see Goods taken out.** You do not have access to the **Warehouse**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **"A stock count already counted this stock after this document. Record a new document instead; if that count is still open, finish or cancel it first."** You cannot cancel this document any more. See [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#counted-since).
