Your :term[stock]{id="stock"} is the goods you have at a :term[point]{id="point"}. Look at it every day to know what to order, and to find goods that are running low.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it.
::

## See what is in stock

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Stock

Open your point, then **Warehouse**, then **Stock**. You see **What is in stock** at this point, and under **All your stock is worth** the value of everything.

### Find a product

Type a name or a barcode in **Search by product name or barcode**.

### See what is running low

Press **Running low**. This shows products where what is left is at or below the **Minimum stock** you set on the product. Press **Everything** to see all of them again.

:::screenshot{src="warehouse/check-stock-1" alt="The stock list with the Everything and Running low buttons"}
:::

### Read the table

For each product you see its **Category**, how much is **In stock**, and, if you filled them in when you received the goods, the **Batch**, the **Place** and **Best before**. To find goods that expire soon, use **Best before from** and **Best before until**.
::

::tip
Set **Minimum stock** on each product. Then it shows under **Running low**, and you get a notification when it gets low: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
::

## See what your stock is worth

Press **See each product** next to **All your stock is worth**. The page **What your stock is worth** shows, for each product, how much is **In stock**, the **Cost of one**, and what it is **Worth**. At the top is the total.

To see what the stock was worth on another day, choose a date in **On the day**. Leave it empty to see the value now. The day counts until its end. Press **Back to stock** to go back.

The value is counted from what the goods cost you, not from your selling price: see [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Nothing in stock yet."** Your stock grows when you add received goods to stock: see [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
- **A delivery is not in the stock.** It is still a draft. Open it and press **Add to stock**.
- **"Nothing is running low."** No product is at or below its **Minimum stock**. Set **Minimum stock** on the products you want to watch.
- **The number is wrong.** Goods can be taken out by a write-off or a move: see [Take goods out of stock](/en/warehouse/write-off) and [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
