A product is anything you sell or keep in stock, from a bottle of water to a bag of flour. Add each one once. After that you can receive it, give it a price and sell it.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it.
::

## Add a product

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Products

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Warehouse**, then **Products**.

### Press New product

Press **New product**. A form opens.

### Fill in the form

- **Name** (required): what you call the product, for example "Sparkling water 0.5 L".
- **Category**: choose one, or add a new one right there with **New category**. This helps you find products later.
- **Description**: anything that helps you tell similar products apart.
- **Barcode**: type it, or leave it empty. If you scan the barcode when you receive goods, Partha can use it to find the product.
- **Unit** (required): **piece**, **kg**, **liter** or **meter**. Choose how you count this product.
- **Minimum stock**: when stock falls to this number or below, the product shows under Running low and you get a notification.
- **VAT** (shown only when your business charges VAT): leave as it is, unless this product has a different :term[VAT]{id="vat"} rate from the rest.

:::screenshot{src="warehouse/add-items-1" alt="The New product form filled in for a bottle of sparkling water"}
:::

### Press Create

Press **Create**. The product is in your list.
::

::note
This only creates the product. It has no stock yet. To add stock, use **Receive delivery**.
::

## Group products into categories

Categories group your products, for example Drinks or Bakery, so they are easy to find. Open **Warehouse**, then **Categories**, press **Add a category**, type a **Name** (and a **Description** if you like) and press **Create**.

## Change or remove a product

Open the product from the list, change what you need and press **Save**. **Remove product** takes it off your list.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Another product already has this barcode."** Each barcode belongs to one product. Find the other product, or type a different barcode.
- **You cannot find a product.** Use the search box at the top of the list. You can search by name, barcode or description.
- **The product has no price at the till.** Set one in **Prices**. Until then, the cashier types the price for each sale.
