When goods go from one :term[point]{id="point"} to another, for example from your warehouse to a shop, record it in Partha. The sending point sends the goods, and the receiving point accepts them when they arrive. So both stocks stay right.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this, at both points. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it. Add the products first: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
::

## Send goods

::steps{level="3"}
### Start the move

Open the point that sends the goods, then **Warehouse**, then **Moved between points**. Press **Move goods**. A new :term[draft]{id="draft"} opens. Your :term[stock]{id="stock"} does not change until the other point accepts the goods.

### Fill in the top of the form

- **From point**: your point. You cannot change it.
- **To point**: choose where the goods go (**Choose where the goods go**).
- **Date**: the date of the move.
- **Document number (optional)** and **Note (optional)**: anything that helps you find this move later.

### Add the products

Scan a barcode or type a name in **Scan or type a product**. For each product, type the **Quantity** you send.

### Press Send goods

Press **Send goods**. Partha asks **Send these goods?** and tells you which point gets them. Confirm. After that the move can no longer be edited.
::

## Accept the goods

When the goods arrive, someone at the receiving point accepts them.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the move

At the receiving point, open **Warehouse**, then **Moved between points**. The move is in the list. Open it.

### Press Add to stock

You see **Accept transfer**: "Press the button when the goods have arrived. They are then added to your stock." If you like, write a **Note (optional)**. Press **Add to stock**.
::

The goods are now in the stock of the receiving point. The sending point loses them at the same moment.

## Fix a mistake

- **While it is a draft**, change anything you like, or press **Delete draft**.
- **After you sent it**, the move cannot be edited. Open it and press **Cancel this transfer**, and write **Why are you cancelling it?** if you like.
  - If the other point has **not accepted** it yet, no stock changes. The move stays in the list, marked as cancelled.
  - If the other point **already accepted** it, the goods go back: the other point takes them out of its stock, and they return to yours. If the other point has already sold some of them, this fails.

::caution
Cancelling a move cannot be undone. If you still need the goods moved, record a new move.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **You cannot choose the point.** The list shows your other points. If it is empty, add another point first: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point).
- **The cancel fails.** The other point has already sold some of the goods. Ask them to check their stock, or record a new move to bring the rest back.
- **You do not see the move at the receiving point.** Open the list again. Check that the sending point pressed **Send goods**, and that you are at the right point.
- **You do not see Moved between points.** You do not have access to the **Warehouse**. Ask the owner of the business.
