A product is anything you sell or keep in stock, from a bottle of water to a T-shirt. A product can have **variants**: the same product in different sizes or colours. Each variant has its own barcode, price and stock.

::note
You need the access **Manage products** to add or change products. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see **Add product**. Products belong to the whole business, so every :term[point]{id="point"} sees the same list. Prices and stock are set for each point.
::

## Add a product

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Products

Open **Warehouse**, then **Products**. You can also open **Business**, then **Products**. Press **Add product**.

### Fill in the basics

- **Name** (required): what you call the product, for example "Sparkling water 0.5 L".
- **Category**: press **Select category** and choose one, or add a new one right there with **New category**. See [Organise products in categories](/en/warehouse/categories).
- **Unit** (required): **piece**, **kg**, **liter** or **meter**. Choose how you count this product. All variants of a product use the same unit.
- **VAT**: leave it as it is, unless this product has a different :term[VAT]{id="vat"} rate from the rest.
- **Sell on till**: turn it off to keep the product out of the till. You can turn it on again at any time.

### Add the price and the barcode

- **Barcode**: scan it, type it, or press **Make a barcode** and Partha makes a free one for you. Each barcode belongs to one product.
- **Price**: the selling price. If your business has several points, you see **Price at** and the name of each point.

A product with no price is saved, but it is not for sale yet.

### Press Save product

Press **Save product**. The product is in your list.
::

::note
The form has no starting stock. To put the first goods in stock, use **Receive stock** (see below).
::

## Sell the same product in sizes or colours

Use variants when customers choose between versions of one product, for example a T-shirt in S, M and L.

::steps{level="3"}
### Add options

On the form, press **Add options**. An option is what tells the variants apart. In **Option**, type a name, for example "Size". In **Values**, type a value and press :kbd{value="Enter"}, for example S, then M, then L.

You can press **Add another option** for a second option, for example Colour. Partha then makes one variant for every combination.

### Fill in each variant

A table of **Variants** appears. For each variant fill in the **Barcode** (or press **Make a barcode**), the **Price**, and turn **Sell on till** on or off.

### Press Save product

A product can have up to 100 variants. If you have more, remove some values.
::

To remove a variant, open its **Actions** menu and choose **Remove variant**. The variant stops selling. Past sales stay.

[]{#still-finishing}

## Saved, still finishing

A product with many variants is saved in several steps. If the connection drops or one step fails, Partha does not lose your work. It shows a message at the top of the product:

- **Saved. Still finishing: …** Partha saved the product, but some variants or prices are not done yet. Those variants show **Finishing**, or **Not for sale yet: no price**. Press **Retry saving**. If you leave the page, the product stays in your list, and you can open it later and press **Retry saving**.
- **Needs your action** means Partha cannot finish a step by itself. The message names the variant and what to change. The most common case: **This barcode is already used by** another product. Change the barcode of that variant (or press **Make a barcode**) and press **Save product** again.

In the list of products, the view **Needs your action** shows the products that wait for you.

[]{#link-existing}

## Link existing products into one product

You may already have the same product as separate items, for example "T-shirt S" and "T-shirt M". Link them, so they are variants of one product. Their barcodes, stock and history stay as they are.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the product

Open the product that should hold the others. To make a new product from existing ones, start with **Add product** and type its **Name** first.

### Press Link existing products

In **Products to link**, search by name or scan a barcode, and pick the products. Under **Options**, name what tells them apart (for example "Size") and type the value of each product. A product that already has options only asks for the values.

### Press Link products

On a new product the button is **Make product**, and you need at least two products. Partha shows **Products linked**.
::

Some things stop a link, and Partha says which: a product is already a variant of another product (unlink it there first), a product is archived (restore it first), or the units differ.

To split a variant off again, open its **Actions** menu and choose **Unlink**. It becomes a product of its own, with its stock and history. Open **Stock and history** in the same menu to see its stock.

::note
When you link products, the name of each product is replaced by the name of the group. To change the name of a linked item later, change the name on the product.
::

## Archive and restore

Archive a product you no longer sell. It stops selling on the till. Stock and history stay.

::steps{level="3"}
### Choose Archive product

Open the product and press **Archive product**. Or open the menu of its row in the list and choose **Archive product**. To archive several, tick them and press **Archive products**.

### Confirm

Press **Archive product**. A message appears with **Undo**. Press **Undo** within a few seconds to bring the product back.
::

::warning
If the product is a group of linked products, archiving it **ungroups** it instead. Each item goes on selling as a product of its own, with its stock and history. Ungrouping cannot be undone with **Undo**. To group them again, link them again.
::

To see archived products, press **Show archived** in the list. Choose **Restore product** on a row, or tick several and press **Restore products**. A restored product comes back as a draft. Check that **Sell on till** is on and that it has a price.

If a variant of the product cannot come back, because its barcode is now used by another product, Partha tells you. Open the product and change the barcode.

[]{#receive-stock}

## Receive stock

The first goods go into stock through a goods receipt, the same as every later delivery.

::steps{level="3"}
### Press Receive stock

Open the product and press **Receive stock**. Or open the menu of its row in the list and choose **Receive stock**. If you work at several points, choose the point (**Receive at** and the name of the point).

### Check the receipt

Partha opens a new draft of **Goods received** with one line for each variant of the product. A variant you received before shows its last cost: **Same as last time**. A variant you never received asks for its price: **Not received before — enter the price.**

### Type the quantity and the cost

For each line type the **Quantity** and the **Purchase price**. A line is saved when you finish typing its price. Your stock does not change yet.

### Add it to stock

Press **Add to stock**. The stock of each variant goes up. To see it, open **Stock** or press **Stock and history** in the variant's menu.
::

If you press **Receive stock** twice, Partha still makes only one draft. Details of the receipt form are in [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This barcode is already used by …".** Each barcode belongs to one product. Find the other product, or type or make a different barcode.
- **You cannot find a product.** Use the search box at the top of the list. You can search by name or barcode. Archived products show only after you press **Show archived**.
- **The product does not sell at the till.** Check that **Sell on till** is on and that the product has a price for that point. You can set prices in **Prices**.
- **You do not see Add product.** You need the access **Manage products**. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
