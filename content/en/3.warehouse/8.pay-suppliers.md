When you add goods from a supplier to stock, Partha remembers what you owe them. Record each payment you make, and you always see what is left to pay, and which payments are late.

::note
Only the owner of the business can do this at first. The owner can let other people see what is owed or record payments: see [Who can do what](#who-can-do-what).
::

## What you owe for a delivery

When you press **Add to stock** on a delivery that has a supplier, Partha records a :term[debt]{id="debt"}: the money you owe that supplier for this delivery. It is the total of the goods, plus the extra costs of the delivery (customs, delivery, loading) that you have not marked as paid to someone else.

- **Pay by**: the day by which you should pay. It is the date you typed in **Pay by (optional)** on the delivery. If you left it empty, it is the date on the paper plus the usual number of days in your country (see [Country differences](#country-differences)).
- **One debt per supplier and currency.** An extra cost with **Paid to (optional)** set to another supplier, for example a delivery company, is a separate debt to that company. An extra cost in another currency is a separate debt in that currency. You pay each one on its own.
- **No supplier, no debt.** A delivery without a supplier records no debt for its goods. Only an extra cost with **Paid to (optional)** set records a debt, to the one it names.
- **Older deliveries.** Deliveries added to stock before Partha started tracking payments to suppliers (November 2026) have no debt in Partha. If you still owe money for one of them, keep track of it outside Partha.

You see the debt on the delivery: open **Warehouse**, then **Goods received**, then the delivery. The section **Payment to the supplier** has one card per debt, with **Amount**, **Paid**, **Left to pay**, **Pay by** and **Supplier invoice no.**

Each card shows its state:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not paid** | Nothing is paid yet. |
| **Part paid** | Some is paid, the rest is still owed. |
| **Paid** | Everything is paid. |
| **Cancelled** | The delivery was cancelled, so nothing is owed for it any more. |

A red **Overdue** mark means something is still owed and the **Pay by** day has passed. Partha counts days in the time zone of your business.

## Record a payment

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the debt

Open the delivery (**Warehouse**, then **Goods received**, then the delivery). Or open **Warehouse**, then **Suppliers**, then **Unpaid**, then the supplier, and press **Pay** next to the delivery.

### Press Record payment

On the card of the debt, press **Record payment**. A form opens on the side. At the top it shows the supplier, the **Amount of the receipt** and what is **Left to pay**.

### Fill in the payment

- **Amount paid**: Partha fills in what is left to pay. You can pay part of it now and the rest later: type the amount you really paid. It cannot be more than what is left.
- **Date of the payment**: the day you paid. Today is filled in. It cannot be a day after today.
- **How you paid**: cash, bank transfer, card and so on. The list depends on your country.
- **Note (optional)**: for example the bank reference, or who took the cash.

<!-- SCREENSHOT TODO warehouse/pay-suppliers-2: the Record payment form filled in. Needs the demo business. -->

### Press Record payment

Press **Record payment**. You see "Payment of … recorded." **Left to pay** goes down, the payment appears under **Payments**, and the state changes to **Part paid** or **Paid**.
::

::tip
A payment is always in the currency of the debt. If you paid a supplier in another currency, record the amount in the currency of the debt.
::

## Fix a payment entered by mistake

A recorded payment is never edited or deleted. If it is wrong (a wrong amount, entered twice, or the money came back), you :term[reverse]{id="reversal"} it and, if needed, record it again correctly.

::steps{level="3"}
### Find the payment

Open the delivery. Under **Payments**, find the payment and press **Reverse**.

### Say why

The window **Reverse this payment?** opens. Type the reason in **Why are you reversing it?**, for example "entered twice". The reason is required.

### Press Reverse payment

Press **Reverse payment**. You see "The payment was reversed. The amount is owed again." The payment stays in the list, marked **reversed**, and a new line with a minus amount and the word **Reversed** is added, so you can always see what happened. Press **Keep it** if you changed your mind.
::

Then record the correct payment, if there is one.

::warning
A reversal is dated today. If today is in a closed period (a past stretch of time the owner has closed), you cannot reverse a payment until the owner opens it again. A reversal itself cannot be reversed: if you reversed a payment by mistake, record the payment again.
::

## Cancel a delivery that has payments

You cannot cancel a delivery while payments to the supplier are recorded for it. First reverse each payment, as above. Then press **Cancel this receipt** (see [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods#fix-a-mistake)). The debt becomes **Cancelled**, and nothing is owed for it any more.

## See what you owe all your suppliers

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Warehouse**, then **Suppliers**, and press **Unpaid** next to **All suppliers**. This view shows the whole business, all points together, not only the point you opened.

- **You owe** and **Overdue**: the totals, one box per currency.
- The table has one line per supplier and currency: **Owed**, **Overdue** (in red), **Next payment by** and **Unpaid receipts**. Suppliers you are late paying come first.

<!-- SCREENSHOT TODO warehouse/pay-suppliers-3: Suppliers, Unpaid view with the totals and the table. Needs the demo business. -->

Press a supplier to see each delivery you have not fully paid. The page **Unpaid:** with the supplier's name lists the deliveries of every point, with **Pay by**, **Amount**, **Paid**, **Left to pay** and **Status**. Use the filters:

- **Status**: **Not fully paid** (shown first), **Paid**, **Cancelled** or **All**.
- **Only overdue**: only what is late.

Press **Pay** to record a payment, or press a line to open the delivery.

You can also open a supplier's debts from **All suppliers**: press the icon **What you owe this supplier** on its line, or press **What you owe** on the supplier's page.

## See how late your payments are

Under the table of the **Unpaid** view, **How late the payments are** shows what was owed on one day, sorted by how many days it was past its pay-by date:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Not due yet** | The pay-by day has not come yet. |
| **1–30 days late** | Late by up to a month. |
| **31–60 days late** | Late by one to two months. |
| **61–90 days late** | Late by two to three months. |
| **Over 90 days late** | Late by more than three months. Talk to these suppliers first. |
| **Total** | Everything owed to the supplier on that day. |

The report shows today by default. To see another day, for example the last day of a month, change **As of**. If nothing was owed that day, it says so.

## Country differences

The number of days Partha gives you to pay when **Pay by (optional)** is empty, and the ways of paying, depend on the country of your business. When you have chosen the supplier of a delivery and left **Pay by (optional)** empty, Partha shows these days and the date they give under the field.

::tabs
  :::tabs-item{label="Tajikistan"}
  - **If Pay by is empty**: 30 days after the date on the paper.
  - **How you paid**: **Cash**, **Bank transfer**, **Mobile wallet**, **Card**, **Other**.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="United Kingdom"}
  - **If Pay by is empty**: 30 days after the date on the paper.
  - **How you paid**: **Bank transfer**, **Card**, **Cash**, **Cheque**, **Other**.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Other countries"}
  - **If Pay by is empty**: 30 days after the date on the paper.
  - **How you paid**: **Bank transfer**, **Cash**, **Card**, **Other**.
  :::
::

These days are what Partha uses, not a rule of law. If you agreed other terms with a supplier, type the date in **Pay by (optional)** when you receive the delivery.

## Who can do what

These are business-wide access items. No role includes them: only the owner has them at first. The owner gives them one by one, under **For the whole business**, in [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).

| Access | Lets a person |
| --- | --- |
| **What you owe suppliers**: Open details | See the section **Payment to the supplier**, the **Unpaid** view and the report |
| **Payments to suppliers**: Add | Record a payment |
| **Payments to suppliers**: Cancel | Reverse a payment |

::note
The **Unpaid** view is under **Warehouse** at a point. A person who sees what is owed also needs **Suppliers** (See the list) or **Goods received** at that point to get there. Without access to suppliers, a supplier's name shows as "Supplier (you cannot see supplier details)".
::

## If something went wrong

**When you record a payment:**

- **"Enter an amount above zero."** Type how much you paid.
- **"Enter the amount as a number, for example 120.50."** Type only digits and one decimal mark, without letters or spaces.
- **"This is more than is left to pay. Enter at most what is left."** You cannot pay more than **Left to pay**. If you really paid more, record what is left, and agree the rest with the supplier.
- **"Enter the date of the payment."** Choose the day you paid.
- **"A payment cannot be dated after today."** Choose today or an earlier day. Record a future payment on the day you make it.
- **"This date is in a closed period. Pick a later date or ask the owner to open the period."** The day you chose is in a period the owner has closed.
- **"Choose how you paid."** Choose a way in **How you paid**.
- **"This way of paying is not offered any more. Choose another one."** Choose another way in **How you paid**.
- **"A payment is always in the currency of the debt. Refresh the page and try again."** Refresh the page and record the amount in the currency of the debt.
- **"This debt was cancelled because its goods receipt was cancelled. There is nothing to pay."** Someone cancelled the delivery. Refresh the page.
- **"The note is too long: at most 500 characters."** Make the **Note (optional)** shorter.
- **"A payment was already recorded with this form, with other details. …"** The form was sent once already. Check the payments of the delivery before you pay again. To record another payment, close the form and press **Record payment** again.
- **"This debt no longer exists. Refresh the page."** Refresh the page.
- **"Could not record the payment. Check your connection and try again."** Check your internet and press **Record payment** again. Then check under **Payments** that the payment is there once.

**When you reverse a payment:**

- **"Today is in a closed period, so a payment cannot be reversed now. Ask the owner to open the period."** A reversal is dated today. Ask the owner.
- **"This payment was already reversed. Refresh the page."** Someone reversed it already.
- **"A reversal cannot be reversed."** Record the payment again instead.
- **"This payment no longer exists. Refresh the page."** Refresh the page.
- **"The reason is too long: at most 500 characters."** Make the reason shorter.
- **"Could not reverse the payment. Check your connection and try again."** Check your internet and try again.

**Other messages:**

- **"Payments to the supplier are recorded for this receipt. Reverse them first …, then cancel the receipt."** See [Cancel a delivery that has payments](#cancel-a-delivery-that-has-payments). If the message tells you to ask the owner, you do not have the access **What you owe suppliers**, so you cannot see those payments: ask the owner of the business to reverse them.
- **"Nothing is owed for this receipt. …"** The delivery has no supplier, or it was added to stock before Partha started tracking payments to suppliers.
- **"Could not load what is owed for this receipt."** or **"Could not load the report."** Press **Try again**.
- **"Unknown supplier"** The supplier was not found, for example it was removed. The debt is still there: you can still pay it.
- **You do not see Unpaid or Payment to the supplier.** You do not have the access **What you owe suppliers**. Ask the owner of the business.
- **You do not see Record payment or Reverse.** You do not have the access **Payments to suppliers**. Ask the owner of the business.
