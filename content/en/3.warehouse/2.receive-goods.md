When a supplier brings goods, record them in Partha. Your :term[stock]{id="stock"} goes up, and Partha remembers what each product cost you, so it can show your profit later.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it. Add your products first: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).
::

## Steps

::steps{level="3"}
### Start a delivery

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Warehouse**, then **Goods received**. Press **Receive delivery**. You can also press **Receive delivery** on the home screen of the point.

A new :term[draft]{id="draft"} opens. Your stock does not change until you finish it.

### Fill in the top of the form

- **From supplier**: who brought the goods. Choose one, or add a new one with **New supplier** (see [Add a supplier](/en/warehouse/suppliers)).
- **Into point**: the point that receives the goods.
- **Date on the paper**: the date written on the supplier's paper.
- **Waybill number** (the number printed on the supplier's waybill): in markets that ask for it, the field has a star (\*) and you cannot add the goods to stock without it. Some markets also show **Power of attorney number**: fill it in only if the delivery came with one. The fields your market asks for come just before your own number.
- **Your own number for this delivery (optional)** and **Note (optional)**: only for you, to find this delivery later. Do not put the supplier's number here: it goes in the starred field. (If your market asks for no paper number, this field is called **Supplier's paper no. (optional)**.)
- **Currency**: change it only if you paid in another currency. Then type the **Rate**: how many of your own currency one unit of that currency cost on the day of the delivery.

### Add the products

Scan a barcode or type a name in **Scan or type a product**. If you type a name, click the product in the list that appears, or press Enter to take the first one. For each product, fill in:

- **Quantity**: how many arrived.
- **Cost per unit**: what you paid for one. If you received the product before, Partha suggests **Same as last time**. If not, type the price.

If a product is not in your list yet, choose **Add it as a new product**. Open **More details** to add a storage place, an expiry date or a batch.

:::screenshot{src="warehouse/receive-goods-1" alt="A delivery with the supplier, three products, their costs and the total"}
:::

### Add extra costs (optional)

Did you pay for customs, delivery or loading? Press **Add a cost**, choose **What for** (**Customs duty**, **Delivery**, **Loading and unloading** or **Other**) and type the **Amount paid**. Choose how to spread it over the goods: **By cost of the goods** or **By quantity**. These costs become part of what the goods cost you.

::note
For accountants: this follows IAS 2. Delivery and customs are part of the cost of the goods.
::

### Add the goods to your stock

Check the **Total**, then press **Add to stock** and confirm. Your stock goes up by the products on the list.
::

Open **Stock** to see the new quantities.

## Fix a mistake

- **Before you press Add to stock**, the delivery is a draft. Change anything you like, or press **Delete draft**.
- **After**, the delivery can no longer be edited. Open it and press **Cancel this receipt** and, if you like, write why. The goods leave your stock again, and the delivery stays in the list marked as cancelled. Then record the delivery again, correctly.

::caution
A cancelled delivery cannot be restored. If you still need the goods in stock, record the delivery again.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Add at least one product first."** A delivery needs at least one line.
- **"Enter the price."** The product was never received before, so there is no earlier cost. Type what you paid.
- **A line repeats another line.** The same product, cost, place and date is listed twice. Join them into one line.
- **"Enter the exchange rate…".** You chose another currency. Type the rate before you add the goods to stock.
