A supplier is a company or person you buy goods from. Add each one once. Then you choose it when you receive a delivery, and Partha can use the supplier's currency.

::note
You need access to the **Warehouse** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it.
::

## Add a supplier

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Suppliers

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Warehouse**, then **Suppliers**.

### Press Add a supplier

Press **Add a supplier**. A form opens.

### Fill in the form

- **Name** (required): the name of the company or person.
- **Email**, **Phone** and **Address**: leave them empty if you do not need them.
- **Currency of purchases**: the currency you pay this supplier in. It is put in a new delivery from this supplier, and you can still change it there. Leave **Same as my business** if you pay in the currency of your business.

:::screenshot{src="warehouse/suppliers-1" alt="The Add a supplier form filled in"}
:::

### Press Create

Press **Create**. The supplier is in your list.
::

::tip
You can also add a supplier while you receive goods. In the delivery, choose **From supplier** and press **New supplier**. See [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
::

## Find a supplier

Use the search box at the top of the list. You can search by name, email or phone.

## Change or remove a supplier

- To change a supplier, press its name in the list, change what you need and press **Save**.
- To remove a supplier, open it and press **Remove supplier**. A message appears with **Undo**, so you can bring the supplier back.

## See what you owe a supplier

Open **Suppliers** and press **Unpaid** next to **All suppliers**. You see what the whole business owes each supplier, and what is late. In **All suppliers**, the icon **What you owe this supplier** on a supplier's line, and the button **What you owe** on the supplier's page, open the deliveries you have not fully paid. You see these only if the owner gave you access to what is owed. See [Pay your suppliers](/en/warehouse/pay-suppliers).

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This field is required."** Type the **Name** of the supplier.
- **You cannot find a supplier.** Search by name, email or phone. If you just removed it, press **Undo** in the message.
- **You do not see Suppliers.** You do not have access to the **Warehouse**. Ask the owner of the business.
