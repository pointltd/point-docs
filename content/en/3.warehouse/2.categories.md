Categories group your products, for example Drinks or Bakery. They help you find a product, and the top-level categories become the tabs on the till.

::note
Categories belong to the whole business, so every :term[point]{id="point"} sees the same ones. You need the access to see, add, change and archive categories. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see **Add category**.
::

## Open the categories

Open **Business**, then **Products**, then the **Categories** tab beside **Products**. You see your categories as a tree, with the number of products in each one.

A category can sit inside another one: a subcategory, and a subcategory inside it. There are three levels at most. At the third level the menu has no **Add subcategory**.

## Add a category

::steps{level="3"}
### Press Add category

A panel opens: **Add category**.

### Type the name

- **Name**: what you call the category. Two categories under the same parent cannot have the same name.
- **Other languages**: if your business works in more than one language, you see a box for each other language. Leave a language empty to show the name above in it.
- **Description**: optional.
- **Under**: choose **Top level**, or the category this one sits in.

### Press Save category

The category is in the tree.
::

To add a subcategory quickly, open the **Actions** menu of a category and choose **Add subcategory**.

::tip
You can also add a category while you add a product. In the **Category** list of the product press **New category**, type the **Name**, choose **Under** if you want, and press **Save category**. The other languages are set later on this page.
::

## Rename or move a category

Open the **Actions** menu of the category. Choose **Rename** to change the name, or **Move to…** to put it under another category. The panel opens (**Change category**): change **Name** or **Under**, and press **Save category**. Partha offers only places where it fits: not inside itself, and not deeper than three levels.

Products stay in the category when you move it.

[]{#till-tabs}

## What the till shows

A category at **Top level** is shown as a tab on the till (the tree says **Shown as a tab on the till**). Subcategories are not tabs.

::note
If your tills are already in use, a new top-level category adds a new tab, and moving a category to the top level adds one too. Tell your cashiers before you change the categories.
::

## Archive a category

::steps{level="3"}
### Choose Archive

Open the **Actions** menu of the category and choose **Archive**.

### Confirm

Press **Archive category**. A message appears with **Undo**. Press **Undo** within a few seconds to bring the category back.
::

::warning
Partha archives only an empty category. If it still has products or subcategories, you see a message such as "still has 7 products and 0 subcategories. Move or archive them first." Move them to another category first (change the **Category** of the products, or move the subcategories), then archive again.
::

There is no list of archived categories yet. If you press **Keep category**, nothing changes. After the message with **Undo** is gone, the app cannot bring the category back yet, so archive only a category you no longer need.

## If something went wrong

- **"Another category under the same parent already has this name."** Choose another name, or put the category under another parent.
- **"The category above this one is archived."** Restore the category above first.
- **You cannot add a third-level subcategory.** Three levels are the most. Put the new category one level up.
- **You do not see Add category.** You need the access to add categories. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
