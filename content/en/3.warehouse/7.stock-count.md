A :term[stock count]{id="stock-count"} is when you count the goods that are really at your :term[point]{id="point"} and compare them with what Partha has. Goods get lost, broken, stolen or written down wrong. A count finds the :term[difference]{id="difference"}, and after someone checks it, Partha corrects your :term[stock]{id="stock"}. Count at least once a month, and every time a number looks wrong.

::note
A count needs two people: one **counts** the goods, another **checks** the result and updates the stock. The person who counted cannot check their own count. In a business where you work alone, you as the owner may do both: see [When you work alone](#when-you-work-alone).
::

## How a count works

1. Someone creates the count and chooses which products to count.
2. The counter counts the goods and sends the result. The counter does not see how many Partha expects.
3. The reviewer sees the difference for each product and decides: accept it, reject it or ask for a recount.
4. The reviewer presses **Update stock**. Only then does your stock change.

## Before you count

- **Record the day's paperwork first.** Add every delivery, write-off and move you already have on paper. If goods were on the shelf and counted, but the delivery is recorded only after the count, Partha adds them twice.
- **Receive the goods that are on the way.** While a product is in a move that was sent but not received yet, the count cannot change its stock. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
- **You may keep selling.** Partha remembers its number for a product at the moment the first person enters it. Sales after that do not spoil the count.
- **Count each product in one go.** If a product sits on two shelves, count both shelves one after the other. If a sale happens between the two shelves, Partha marks the product, and the reviewer decides.
- **Count together.** Several people can count the same count at the same time, for example one person per shelf. Partha adds up what they enter.
- **Split a big point.** One count holds up to 100 products. Make one count per category, or choose the products of one shelf or room. Several counts can run at the same point at the same time, but one product can be in only one open count.

[]{#blind-counting}

## Why the counter does not see the numbers

Partha uses a :term[blind count]{id="blind-count"}: the counter sees the list of products, but not how many Partha expects, not the difference and not the cost. This way the counter writes what is really there, and cannot simply copy the number from the screen or hide a shortage.

::warning
A blind count works only if the counter cannot see the stock anywhere else in Partha. Give counters the **Stock counter** role: it lets them count, but not see **Stock** or the deliveries and write-offs. A person with the **Cashier** or **Warehouse manager** role can see the stock on other pages. When such a person counts, the review screen warns you that the count was not blind for them. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Create a count

You need the **Add** and **Edit** access for **Stock counts**. The **Warehouse manager** role has it.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Stock counts

Open your point, then **Warehouse**, then **Stock counts**. Press **New count**. A new :term[draft]{id="draft"} opens.

### Say what to count

- **Date of the count**: the day you count the goods.
- **What to count**: choose **Part of the stock** and pick categories or products, or choose **Everything at this point**.

With **Everything at this point**, Partha makes the list itself: every product you have in stock, and every product that moved in the last 90 days. You can count everything only if the point has 100 products or fewer. Otherwise, make one count per category.

### Add the commission and the basis, if you need them

Under **Commission** you can write who takes part in the count, and under **Basis** why you count, for example "Order No. 3 of 5 November". This is for your own paperwork. Partha does not check it, and it does not turn the count into an official form for your country.

### Press Start counting

Press **Start counting**. Partha makes the list of products and opens the count for counting. If you chose **Part of the stock**, Partha also adds a few products you did not choose: see [Spot-check products](#hidden-extra-products).
::

## Count the goods

You need the **Count** access for **Stock counts**. The **Stock counter** role has it.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the count

Open your point, then **Warehouse**, then **Stock counts**. Open the count you were asked to do and press **Count**.

### Count each product

Use **Scan or type a product**:

- **Scan** the barcode of each item. Every scan adds 1.
- **Or type** the name, type how many you counted, and press **Add**.

**My count** shows your total for each product. If a product sits in two places, enter each place separately: Partha adds them up. If you see **Someone else counted this product too**, a colleague counted it as well, and Partha adds their number to yours.

If a product on the list is not there at all, type **0** and press **Add**. A product you leave empty is not counted, and its stock does not change.

### Fix your own mistakes

Press **Show my entries**, then **Remove** next to the wrong entry, and enter the right number. You can remove only your own entries. The reviewer still sees what you removed.

### Add a product that is not on the list

If you scan a product that is not on the list, Partha says so. Press **Add to the count**: Partha adds it and marks it for the reviewer. You cannot remove products from the list.

### Press Submit count

When you are done, press **Submit count**. Partha asks **Submit your count?** and tells you how many products were not counted. Their stock will not change. Go back and count them, or submit anyway.
::

## Check the count and update the stock

You need the **Check and approve** access for **Stock counts**, and the **Update stock** access to change the stock. The **Warehouse manager** role has both. You cannot check a count that you counted in yourself, unless you are the owner.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the count that waits for a check

Open **Stock counts** and open a count with the status **Waiting for check**. For each product you see **Expected** (the number in Partha), **Counted**, **Difference** and **Cost of difference**. On top you see the totals: **Missing**, **Extra**, **Waiting for a decision** and **To look at**.

Some products have a mark:

- **Added while counting**: a counter added the product; it was not on the list.
- **Spot check**: Partha added the product without telling the counter. See [Spot-check products](#hidden-extra-products).
- **Stock moved while it was counted**: the product was sold, received or moved between the first and the last time it was counted. The difference may be wrong. Ask for a recount, or accept it with a reason.
- **Sold or received after the count**: this is normal. The difference is still right, because Partha compared with its number at the moment of the count.
- **On transfer TR-…, not received yet**: receive or cancel that move before you update the stock, or reject this product and count it again later.
- **Big difference**: the difference is over the limits in your business settings. Another person must recount the product before you can accept it. See [Change your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

### Decide for each product with a difference

- **Accept**: the difference is real. Under **Choose a reason**, pick one. For missing goods: **Theft**, **Damaged**, **Expired**, **Counting mistake** or **Not known**. For extra goods: **Received without a document**, **Counting mistake** or **Not known**.
- **Reject**: Partha keeps its own number for this product, and nothing changes.

You can **Change** a decision until you update the stock. You never type the quantity yourself: Partha always uses the last count.

For extra goods, you can type the **Cost of one unit**. If you do not, Partha uses the product's current :term[cost]{id="cost"}, or the cost of its last delivery.

### Ask for a recount, if needed

Tick the products and press **Ask for a recount**. Under **What should be recounted, and why?** write the reason. A different person must count them again: not the one who counted them before (only the owner may recount products they counted before). Partha also reopens a few other products, so the counter cannot tell which numbers were off. The count shows **Recount** until the :term[recount]{id="recount"} is submitted, then it comes back to you with the new numbers.

### Press Update stock

When every product with a difference has a decision, press **Update stock** and confirm. The count gets the next number of your point (IC-000001, IC-000002 and so on), its status becomes **Stock updated**, your stock changes, and the count can no longer be edited.
::

## What changes in your stock and cost

- **Missing goods** leave your stock. Their cost is taken from your stock the same way as for a sale or a write-off, and it becomes a loss of the period of the count.
- **Extra goods** come into your stock at the cost you typed, or the product's current cost, or the cost of its last delivery.
- **Products you rejected or did not count** do not change.
- Partha adds or removes only the difference, from the stock you have now. Example: Partha has 10 bottles, the counter counts 8, and a customer buys 1 before you update the stock. After the update, Partha has 7. The sale is kept, and the 2 missing bottles are taken out.

::note
For accountants: a shortage is an expense of the period when you find it, and a surplus is added to stock at cost. This follows IAS 2.
::

## Spot-check products

When you count **Part of the stock**, Partha adds 5 more products you did not choose. The counter sees them as ordinary products on the list and cannot tell them apart. Partha does the same when you ask for a recount. The reviewer sees them marked **Spot check**.

Why: the person who chooses what to count could leave out exactly the goods that are missing. Spot-check products make that hard. You can change their number, or set it to 0, in [your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

## Rules for the owner

### Large differences

If the total difference of a count is over the limit you set, only the owner can update the stock. The total adds up missing and extra goods without letting one cancel out the other, so cheap extra goods cannot hide an expensive shortage. You can turn this rule off in [your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

### When you work alone

If you are the owner, you may check a count you counted yourself and update the stock. Partha shows **You counted this yourself** and asks **Why are you approving your own count?** The count is marked **Self-approved** in the list and on the count. If you counted alone, you can also accept a big difference without a recount, and you may recount products yourself. This is meant for a business with one person. When you have staff, let someone else count.

[]{#reverse-a-count}

### Undo a count

A count that updated the stock is never edited or deleted. If it was wrong, the owner can undo it: open the count, press **Undo this count** and answer **Why are you undoing it?** Partha undoes what the count changed: missing goods come back into your stock, and extra goods go out again. Sales and other documents since the count stay as they are. The count stays in the list with the status **Undone**.

::caution
Undoing a count is final. It works only for the whole count, and it does not work when:

- a later count already checked the same products. Make a new count instead;
- some extra goods from this count are already sold, so they cannot be taken out again. Make a new count instead.
::

[]{#counted-since}

### A document a count already checked

Once a count has updated the stock, you cannot cancel a delivery, a write-off, a move or an earlier count whose goods it already counted. The count has already put your stock right. Record a new document instead, for example a new write-off.

## Cancel a count

- **A draft**: press **Delete draft**.
- **A count that has not updated the stock yet**: open it, press **Cancel this count** and answer **Why are you cancelling it?** (or press **Keep it** to go back). Nothing changes in your stock. The count stays in the list with the status **Cancelled**. You need the **Edit** access for this.

## See who counted what

Open **Stock counts** and press **Report**. You need the **Check and approve** access. For the days you choose, **By product** shows which products were missing or extra most often, and **By person** shows what each person counted. Use it to find where goods go missing and to plan the next counts. A difference is not proof that a person did something wrong: talk to them first.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This product is already on another open count."** One product can be in only one open count. Finish or cancel the other count, or leave the product out of this one. If you are counting, count it on the other count.
- **"A count can have at most 100 products."** Split the point by category into several counts. If this happens while you add a product, tell the person who created the count.
- **"This product is not being counted now."** During a recount, only the products sent for a recount are open.
- **"You can only remove what you counted yourself."** Ask the person who made the entry to remove it.
- **You cannot submit.** Nobody has counted anything yet. Count at least one product.
- **"This can't be done while the count has this status."** Someone else has already submitted, updated or cancelled the count. Reload the page to see where it stands.
- **"You counted on this document, so someone else must check it and update stock."** Ask another person who has the **Check and approve** access.
- **"Someone else must recount this product: you counted it last time."** Ask a colleague to recount it.
- **"The difference is big. Ask someone else to recount this product first."** The product is over your limit. Ask for a recount by another person first.
- **"Accept or reject this difference first."** Every product with a difference needs a decision before you update the stock.
- **"The difference is above your limit: only the business owner can update stock."** Ask the owner to update the stock.
- **"Write why you are approving your own count."** You counted in this count yourself. Write the reason.
- **"This product is on a transfer that has not been received yet."** Receive or cancel that move first, or reject this product and count it again later. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
- **There is not enough stock.** The product was sold after the count, and now there is less than is missing. Ask for a recount of that product.
- **"Enter the cost of one unit. This product has no cost yet."** Partha does not know what this product cost you. Type the **Cost of one unit**.
- **"This cost is in a different currency from the stock of this product."** Type the cost in the currency in which this product is already in stock.
- **"This date is in a closed period."** Pick a later date, or ask the owner to open the period.
- **"A stock count already checked these products after this document."** You tried to cancel a delivery, write-off or move. Record a new document instead. See [A document a count already checked](#counted-since).
- **You do not see Stock counts.** You do not have access to it. Ask the owner of the business.
