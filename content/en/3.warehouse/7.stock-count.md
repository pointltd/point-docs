A :term[stock count]{id="stock-count"} is when you count the goods that are really at your :term[point]{id="point"} and compare them with what Partha has. Goods get lost, broken, stolen or written down wrong. A count finds the :term[difference]{id="difference"}, and after someone checks it, Partha corrects your :term[stock]{id="stock"}. Count at least once a month, and every time a number looks wrong.

::note
A count needs two people: one **counts** the goods, another **checks** the result and posts it. The person who counted cannot check their own count. In a business where you work alone, you as the owner may do both: see [When you work alone](#when-you-work-alone).
::

## How a count works

1. Someone creates the count and chooses which products to count.
2. The counter counts the goods and sends the result. The counter does not see how many Partha expects.
3. The reviewer sees the difference for each product and decides: accept it, reject it or ask for a recount.
4. The reviewer posts the count. Only then does your stock change.

## Before you count

- **Record the day's paperwork first.** Add every delivery, write-off and move you already have on paper. If goods were on the shelf and counted, but the delivery is recorded only after the count, Partha adds them twice.
- **Receive the goods that are on the way.** A product that is in a move that was sent but not received yet cannot be posted. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
- **You may keep selling.** Partha remembers its number for a product at the moment the counter enters it. Sales after that do not spoil the count.
- **Count each product in one go.** If a product sits on two shelves, count both shelves one after the other. If a sale happens between the two shelves, Partha marks the product, and the reviewer decides.
- **Count together.** Several people can count the same count at the same time, for example one person per shelf. Partha adds up what they enter.
- **Split a big point.** One count holds up to 100 products. Make one count per category, or choose the products of one shelf or room. Several counts can run at the same point at the same time, but one product can be in only one open count.

[]{#blind-counting}

## Why the counter does not see the numbers

Partha uses a :term[blind count]{id="blind-count"}: the counter sees the list of products, but not how many Partha expects, not the difference and not the cost. This way the counter writes what is really there, and cannot simply copy the number from the screen or hide a shortage.

::warning
A blind count works only if the counter cannot see the stock anywhere else in Partha. Give counters the **Stock counter** role: it lets them count, but not see **Stock** or the deliveries and write-offs. A person with the **Cashier** or **Warehouse manager** role can see the stock on other pages. When such a person counts, the review screen warns you. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Create a count

You need the **Add** and **Edit** access for **Stock counts**. The **Warehouse manager** role has it.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Stock counts

Open your point, then **Warehouse**, then **Stock counts**. Press **New count**. A new :term[draft]{id="draft"} opens.

### Say what to count

- **Date**: the day you count the goods.
- **What to count**: the whole point, or some categories and products.

If you choose the whole point, Partha makes the list itself: every product you have in stock, and every product that moved in the last 90 days. You can choose the whole point only if it has 100 products or fewer. Otherwise, make one count per category.

### Add the people and the basis, if you need them

You can write who takes part in the count, and the basis for it, for example "Order No. 3 of 5 November". This is for your own paperwork. Partha does not check it, and it does not turn the count into an official form for your country.

### Press Start counting

Press **Start counting**. Partha makes the list of products and opens the count for counting. If you chose some categories or products, Partha also adds a few products you did not choose: see [Hidden extra products](#hidden-extra-products).
::

## Count the goods

You need the **Count** access for **Stock counts**. The **Stock counter** role has it.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the count

Open your point, then **Warehouse**, then **Stock counts**, and open the count you were asked to do.

### Count each product

Scan the barcode or type the name in **Scan or type a product**. Type how many you counted, then go to the next product.

- If a product sits in two places, enter each place separately. Partha adds them up.
- If a product on the list is not there at all, enter **0**. A product you leave empty is not counted, and its stock does not change.

### Fix your own mistakes

If you typed a wrong number, remove your entry and enter the right one. You can remove only your own entries. The reviewer still sees what you removed.

### Add a product that is not on the list

If you find a product that is not on the list, scan or type it all the same. Partha adds it to the count and marks it for the reviewer. You cannot remove products from the list.

### Press Submit count

When you are done, press **Submit count**. If some products are not counted yet, Partha tells you how many. You can go back and count them, or submit anyway: those products keep the stock they have.
::

## Check the count and post it

You need the **Review** access for **Stock counts**, and the **Post** access to post. The **Warehouse manager** role has both. You cannot check or post a count that you counted in yourself, unless you are the owner.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the submitted count

Open **Stock counts** and open the count. For each product you see the number in Partha, the number counted, the difference and what the difference costs.

Some products have a mark:

- **Added by the counter**: the product was not on the list.
- **Moved while counting**: the product was sold, received or moved between the first and the last time it was counted. The difference may be wrong. Ask for a recount, or accept it with a reason.
- **Changed since the count**: the product was sold or received after it was counted. This is normal. The difference is still right, because Partha compared with its number at the moment of the count.
- **In a move on the way**: finish or cancel that move before you post, or reject this product and count it again later.
- **Over your limit**: the difference is bigger than the limits in your business settings. Another person must recount the product before you can accept it. See [Change your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

### Decide for each product with a difference

- **Accept**: the difference is real. Choose a reason. For missing goods: theft, damage, expired, counting mistake or unknown. For extra goods: a delivery that was not recorded, counting mistake or unknown. You can add a note.
- **Reject**: Partha keeps its own number for this product, and nothing changes.
- **Ask for a recount**: see the next step.

You never type the quantity yourself. Partha always uses the last count.

For extra goods, you can type what one unit cost you. If you do not, Partha uses the product's current :term[cost]{id="cost"}, or the cost of its last delivery.

### Ask for a recount, if needed

Choose the products, press **Ask for a recount** and write why. A different person must count them again: not the one who counted them before (only the owner may recount their own products). Partha also reopens a few other products, so the counter cannot tell which numbers were off. When the :term[recount]{id="recount"} is submitted, the count comes back to you with the new numbers.

### Press Post count

When every product with a difference has a decision, press **Post count** and confirm. The count gets the next number of your point (IC-000001, IC-000002 and so on), your stock changes, and the count can no longer be edited.
::

## What changes in your stock and cost

- **Missing goods** leave your stock. Their cost is taken from your stock the same way as for a sale or a write-off, and it becomes a loss of the period of the count.
- **Extra goods** come into your stock at the cost you typed, or the product's current cost, or the cost of its last delivery.
- **Products you rejected or did not count** do not change.
- Partha adds or removes only the difference, from the stock you have now. Example: Partha has 10 bottles, the counter counts 8, and a customer buys 1 before you post. After posting, Partha has 7. The sale is kept, and the 2 missing bottles are taken out.

::note
For accountants: a shortage is an expense of the period when you find it, and a surplus is added to stock at cost. This follows IAS 2.
::

## Hidden extra products

When you start a count of some categories or products, Partha adds 5 more products you did not choose. They look like any other product on the list, and the counter cannot tell them apart. Partha does the same when you ask for a recount.

Why: the person who chooses what to count could leave out exactly the goods that are missing. The extra products make that hard. The reviewer sees which products were added. You can change the number of extra products, or set it to 0, in [your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

## Rules for the owner

### Large differences

If the total difference of a count is over the limit you set, only the owner can post it. The total adds up missing and extra goods without letting one cancel out the other, so cheap extra goods cannot hide an expensive shortage. You can turn this rule off in [your business settings](/en/business/business-settings#stock-count-limits).

### When you work alone

If you are the owner, you may check and post a count you counted yourself. Partha asks you to write why, and marks the count as approved by the owner in the list and on the count. If you counted alone, you can also accept a difference that is over your limit without a recount, and you may recount products yourself. This is meant for a business with one person. When you have staff, let someone else count.

[]{#reverse-a-count}

### Reverse a posted count

A posted count is never edited or deleted. If it was wrong, the owner can reverse it: open the count, press **Reverse this count** and write why. Partha undoes what the count changed: missing goods come back into your stock, and extra goods go out again. Sales and other documents since the count stay as they are. The count stays in the list, marked as reversed.

::caution
Reversing cannot be undone. It works only for the whole count, and it does not work when:

- a later count already checked the same products. Record a new count instead;
- some extra goods from this count are already sold, so they cannot be taken out again. Record a new count instead.
::

[]{#counted-since}

### A document a count already checked

Once a count is posted, you cannot cancel a delivery, a write-off, a move or an earlier count whose goods it already counted. The count has already put your stock right. Record a new document instead, for example a new write-off.

## Cancel a count

- **A draft**: you can delete it.
- **A started or submitted count**: open it, press **Cancel this count** and write why. Nothing changes in your stock. The count stays in the list, marked as cancelled.

## See who counted what

Open **Stock counts**, then the count report. You need the **Review** access. The report shows, for the days you choose, which products were short or extra most often and what each counter counted. Use it to find where goods go missing and to plan the next counts. A difference is not proof that a person did something wrong: talk to them first.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **You cannot start the count: a product is already in another count.** One product can be in only one open count. Finish or cancel the other count, or remove the product from this one.
- **There are too many products.** One count holds up to 100 products. Split the point by category into several counts.
- **You cannot add a product that is not on the list.** The product is already in another open count: count it there. Or this count already has 100 products: tell the person who created the count.
- **The app says the count is not in the right status.** Someone else has already submitted, posted or cancelled it. Open the list of counts again to see where it stands.
- **You cannot enter a quantity for a product.** The product is not open for counting now. During a recount, only the products sent for a recount are open.
- **You cannot remove an entry.** You can remove only your own entries.
- **You cannot submit.** Nobody has counted anything yet. Count at least one product.
- **You cannot check or post the count.** You counted in it yourself. Ask another person who has the **Review** access.
- **You cannot recount a product.** You counted it last time. Another person must recount it.
- **You cannot accept a product: it needs a recount.** The difference is over your limit. Ask for a recount by another person first.
- **You cannot post: a product has no decision.** Accept, reject or recount every product with a difference.
- **You cannot post: only the owner can.** The total difference is over your limit. Ask the owner to post it.
- **The owner is asked for a reason.** You counted in this count yourself. Write why you post it alone.
- **You cannot post: a product is in a move on the way.** Receive or cancel that move first, then post. Or reject this product and count it again later. See [Move goods between points](/en/warehouse/move-goods).
- **You cannot post: there is not enough stock.** The product was sold after the count, and now there is less than the shortage. Ask for a recount of that product.
- **You cannot post: extra goods need a cost.** Partha does not know what this product cost you. Type the cost for one unit.
- **You cannot post: the currency does not match.** Type the cost in the currency in which this product is already in stock.
- **You cannot post or reverse: the period is closed.** The date is in a period that is closed for changes. Ask the owner or your accountant.
- **You cannot cancel a delivery, write-off or move.** A posted count already checked those goods. Record a new document instead. See [A document a count already checked](#counted-since).
- **You do not see Stock counts.** You do not have access to it. Ask the owner of the business.
