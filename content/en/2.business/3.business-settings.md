Here you change what Partha knows about your business as a whole: its name and time zone. The VAT, cost and stock count settings are on the same screen.

::note
You need access to the details of the business. Ask the owner if you do not see **Business settings**, or cannot press **Save**.
::

## Change the name or time zone

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Business settings

Open your business, then **Business settings**. Under **Info** you see the details.

### Change what you need

- **Name** (required): the name of your business.
- **Country**: you cannot change it. It was chosen when you created the business.
- **Time zone**: used to show dates and times correctly. Choose the time zone of the place where you work.

:::screenshot{src="business/business-settings-1" alt="Business settings: name, country and time zone"}
:::

### Press Save

Press **Save**. The new details are saved.
::

## Other settings on the same screen

- **VAT**: say whether your business charges VAT. See [Set up VAT](/en/business/set-up-vat).
- **Cost calculation method**: choose how Partha counts what the goods you sell cost you. See [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works).
- **Stock counts**: say when a difference found by a stock count is big. See [Stock counts: big differences](#stock-count-limits) below.

These settings apply to all your :term[points]{id="point"}.

[]{#stock-count-limits}

## Stock counts: big differences

On the **Stock counts** card you say when a difference found by a [stock count](/en/warehouse/stock-count) is big. These settings apply to all your points. Partha keeps a record of every change: who made it, when, and the old and new values.

- **Big difference: at least this many units**, **Big difference: at least this % of the expected quantity** and **Big difference: at least this cost**: fill in the ones you want and leave the others empty. A product gets the **Big difference** mark when any one of them is reached. Then another person must recount it before it can be accepted. The percent does not apply when Partha expects 0 of the product.
- **Whole count: differences add up to at least**: the total difference of the count in money. Missing and extra goods are added up, so extra goods never cancel out missing ones.
- **Only the owner may update stock after a count with a big difference**: on by default. When it is on, only the owner can update the stock after a count whose total is over the limit. Turn it off if you are often not there to do it.
- **Spot-check products added to each count**: how many products Partha adds, without telling the counter, to each count of part of the stock and to each recount. 5 by default, up to 50, and never more than 100 products in a count. 0 turns it off. See [Spot-check products](/en/warehouse/stock-count#hidden-extra-products).

Money limits are in the currency of your business. A product whose cost is in another currency always gets the **Big difference** mark. If you set no limits, no product is ever over a limit. Press **Save** when you are done.

## Delete the business

At the bottom, under **Delete this business**, **Delete business** removes the business.

::caution
Deleting a business cannot be undone. All data of this business will be lost. The app asks you to confirm. Only do this if you are sure.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **You cannot change the country.** It cannot be changed after the business is created. Create a new business with the right country, then delete the wrong one.
- **You cannot press Save.** You do not have access to change the business. Ask the owner.
- **You do not see Delete business.** Only people with that access see it. Ask the owner.
