A business is your company in Partha. You add the places where you work to it (shops, warehouses, cafes) and your team. You create it once, at the start.

::note
You need an account first. If you do not have one, press **Get started** on the Partha start page and confirm your email: see [Your profile and sign-in](/en/business/your-profile).
::

## Steps

::steps{level="3"}
### Start creating the business

If this is your first time, Partha shows **Create your first business**. Press **Create a business**.

If you already have a business, open the list of your businesses and press **Create a business** next to them.

### Type the name

In **Name**, type the name of your company, for example "Dilbar Trade".

### Check the country

Partha has already chosen a **Country**. Check that it is yours. Type in the box to search the list.

::caution
Choose carefully. The country cannot be changed later, and it sets your taxes and your currency.
::

### Check the time zone

Partha picks the **Time zone** that fits your country. Change it only if your business works in another one. It makes dates and times show correctly.

### Choose how to count your cost

Under **Cost calculation method**, choose how Partha works out what the goods you sell cost you. It decides your profit. If you are not sure, keep **Average (simpler)**. The other choice is **Oldest deliveries first (FIFO)**. See [How Partha counts your cost](/en/reports/how-cost-works).

::caution
Choose carefully. After your first stock movement, this cannot be changed.
::

### Press Create

Press **Create**. Your business is ready. Next, Partha asks you to add your first :term[point]{id="point"}.

:::
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"This field is required."** Fill in the **Name**, the **Country** and the **Time zone**. All three are needed.
- **You cannot find your country.** The list only shows the countries where Partha is available.
- **A message says the business is created but the cost method was not saved.** Your business works and uses **Average (simpler)**. Open **Business settings** and choose again, before your first stock movement.
- **You chose the wrong country.** It cannot be changed. Create a new business with the right country, then delete the wrong one: open its **Business settings** and press **Delete business**.

::caution
Deleting a business cannot be undone. All its data is lost.
::
