Your account is you in Partha: the email and password you sign in with. One account can work in many businesses. Here you create the account, sign in and out, and find your profile.

## Create your account

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the sign-up page

At the top of the Partha start page, press **Get started**. You see the form **Create an account**.

### Fill in the form

- **First name** (required)
- **Last name** (you can leave it empty)
- **Email** (required): an address you can open now.
- **Password** (required): at least 8 characters.
- **Confirm password** (required): type the same password again.

### Press Create account

By pressing it you accept the **Terms of service** and the **Privacy policy**. Both are linked under the button.

### Confirm your email

Partha shows **Check your email** and sends a link to your address. Open the link to finish creating your account. It can take a minute. If you do not see the email, look in your spam folder.
::

Or press the Google button under the form to continue with your Google account. You see it only when it is available.

## Sign in

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the sign-in page

You see **Sign in to your account**.

### Type your email and password

Type your **Email** and **Password**.

If you forget your password, press **Forgot your password?** under the password field. If you signed up with Google you have no password: use the Google button.

### Press Sign in

Partha opens your businesses.
::

## Find your profile

Open the account menu and press **Profile**. The page **Your profile** shows your **Name** and your **Email**.

:::screenshot{src="business/your-profile-1" alt="The Your profile page with the name and email"}
:::

::note
You cannot change your name, email or password in the app yet. The page only shows them.
::

In the same menu you can change the **Language** and the **Colour theme** (**Light**, **Dark** or **Same as device**). **Log out** is there too.

::tip
Working at a shared cash desk? Your PIN is not part of your profile. You set it at the cash desk: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
::

## Log out

Open the account menu and press **Log out**. Do it on a shared computer, so the next person does not use your account.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Invalid email or password".** Check both and try again. Make sure you type the email you signed up with.
- **"An account with this email already exists."** You already have an account. Go to the sign-in page and sign in.
- **"Passwords do not match."** Type the same password in **Password** and **Confirm password**.
- **"Use at least 8 characters."** Choose a longer password.
- **The email with the link does not come.** Wait a minute, then check your spam folder. On **Check your email**, press **Send the link again**. You can ask for a new link once a minute.
- **"This link does not work".** The link is old or was already used. Sign in, and Partha sends you a new one.
- **The Google button does not work.** Google may not be answering, or it cannot confirm that the email is yours. Wait a minute and try again, or sign up with your email and password.
- **"Google sign-in does not work inside this app".** Telegram, Instagram and similar apps block Google sign-in. Press **Copy link**, open Chrome or Safari and paste the link there. Or tap the ⋯ or share button in the app and choose **Open in browser**. You can also sign in with your email.
- **The Google window does not open.** Your browser may block pop-up windows. Press **Google window did not open? Continue on this page**, then press the Google button again: Google now opens on the same page instead of a new window.
- **You forgot your password.** Press **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page and write to the contact shown there. The app cannot reset it by itself yet.
- **"You were signed out. Sign in again."** Your session ended. Sign in again.
