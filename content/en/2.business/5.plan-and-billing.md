The **Plan** page is where Partha will show which plan your business uses and what you pay. Today it only tells you that Partha is free while it is in beta. This page tells you what you can and cannot do there, so you do not look for it in vain.

::note
Every member of the business sees **Plan** in the **Business** menu. You do not need special access to open it.
::

## What you see today

::steps{level="3"}
### Open your business

Open your business and choose **Business** in the menu.

### Choose Plan

Press **Plan**. The page opens with the title **Subscription**. Under it you see your current plan: **Free while Partha is in beta**.
::

That is all the page shows now. You pay nothing today. You cannot choose a plan, see a price, see an invoice or pay on it. Partha does not take payments in the app today.

::tip
Because there is nothing to set up, nothing in your business depends on it. You can use all the other parts of Partha as usual.
::

## What it will show later

When plans and billing are ready, they will be on this page, and this guide will describe them. We will announce it in [What's new](/en/help/whats-new). We do not publish prices or limits here until they are real.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **The page shows only a short note.** This is expected: there is nothing to set up or pay yet.
- **You do not see Plan in the menu.** Open your business first. **Plan** is in the **Business** menu, not in a point.
