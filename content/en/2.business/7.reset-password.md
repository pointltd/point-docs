If you forgot your password, you do not need help from anyone. Partha emails you a link, and you choose a new password with it.

::note
Signed up with Google? Then you have no password. Press the Google button on the sign-in page instead.
::

## Steps

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the sign-in page

Under the password field, press **Forgot your password?**. If you already typed your email, Partha keeps it.

### Ask for the link

Type your **Email** and press **Send the link**. Partha shows **Check your email**. It shows the same message for every address, so nobody can use it to find out who has an account.

### Open the email

Look for the email **Reset your Partha password**. It comes in your language and can take a minute. If you do not see it, look in your spam folder. The link works for 1 hour and only once.

### Choose a new password

The link opens **Choose a new password**. Type the **New password** (at least 8 characters) and the same again in **Confirm password**. Press **Save password**.

### Sign in

Partha shows **Password changed**. Press **Go to sign in** and sign in with the new password.
::

::warning
Saving a new password signs you out on all your devices, so nobody who had your old password stays in. Sign in again on each device.
::

## If something went wrong

- **No email comes.** Wait a few minutes, check your spam folder, and check that you typed the email you signed up with. You can ask for a new link once a minute. Only the newest link works.
- **"This link does not work".** The link is older than 1 hour or was already used. Press **Send a new link**.
- **"Too many attempts".** Wait a minute and try again.
- **"Use at least 8 characters" or "Use at most 72 bytes".** Choose a password that is longer, or shorter. Letters in Cyrillic take two bytes each, so about 36 fit.
