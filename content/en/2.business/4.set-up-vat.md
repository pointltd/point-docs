If your business is registered for :term[VAT]{id="vat"}, tell Partha. Then your receipts show the VAT. If it is not, leave it off, and Partha works out no VAT anywhere.

::note
You need access to the details of the business to change this. Ask the owner if the switch is grey. VAT rules differ in every country, so Partha takes the name and the rates of the taxes from your country.
::

## Turn VAT on

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Business settings

Open your business, then **Business settings**. Find **VAT**.

### Turn on the switch

Turn on **My business charges VAT**. The app explains it: "Turn this on if you are registered for VAT. Receipts then show the VAT. Off: no VAT is worked out anywhere."

### Type your tax number

Type your **Tax number** (required). It is printed on receipts.

### Choose the usual rate (if you see it)

If your country has more than one VAT rate, you see **Usual VAT rate**. It is used for every product that has no VAT rate of its own. If your country has one rate, you do not see this field.

### Press Save

Press **Save**.
::

## What changes

- Receipts show the VAT, and the tax number.
- Under the switch, the app says how your country counts it: either "In your country, shelf prices already include VAT. The receipt shows how much of the total is VAT.", or "In your country, VAT is added on top of the shelf price at the till."
- Profit is counted on the price without VAT: see [See your profit](/en/reports/profit).
- A product can have its own VAT rate. Change it when you add the product: see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items).

::tip
You are not sure if you are registered for VAT? Ask your accountant, or the tax office. Do not turn it on if you are not.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **The Tax number field is empty and it will not save.** The tax number is required when the switch is on.
- **The switch is grey.** You do not have access to change the business. Ask the owner.
- **You do not see Usual VAT rate.** Your country has one VAT rate, so there is nothing to choose.
