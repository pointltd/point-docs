A :term[point]{id="point"} is one place where you work: a shop, a warehouse or a cafe. Each point has its own stock, its own cash desk and its own sales. Add one for every place you have.

::note
Only people who may add points to the business can do this, usually the owner. If you see "You don't have access to any point yet", ask the owner to add you to a point.
::

## Steps

::steps{level="3"}
### Open your business

Open your business. You see **Your points**. If you have none yet, Partha shows **Add your first point**.

### Press Add a point

Press **Add a point**. A form opens.

:::screenshot{src="business/add-point-1" alt="The business overview with the Add a point button"}
:::

### Type the name

In **Name**, type what you call this place, for example "Main shop" or "Central warehouse".

### Press Create

Press **Create**. The point appears in the list. Open it to start working: add products, receive goods and sell.

:::screenshot{src="business/add-point-2" alt="The Add a point form with the point's name"}
:::
::

[]{#apps}

## Switch off what a point does not use

A point can use the **Warehouse** and the **Cash desk**. A warehouse that never sells does not need the cash desk.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the point settings

Open the point, then **Point settings**.

### Switch off the app

Under **Apps at this point**, switch off the one this point does not use. Nothing is deleted, and you can switch it on again at any time.

:::screenshot{src="business/add-point-3" alt="Point settings with the Warehouse and Cash desk switches"}
:::
::

## Rename or delete a point

In **Point settings**, change the **Name** and press **Change**.

::caution
The delete button in the **Danger zone** removes the point for good, with all its data. This cannot be undone.
::
