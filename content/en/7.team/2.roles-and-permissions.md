When you add a person, you choose what they can do. You do it by **role** for each point, and you can add or remove single items. This page lists everything you can allow.

## Roles

A role is a ready set of access for one point. Choose it in the **Role** list of a point. You can then change single items, and the role becomes **Custom**. When we add an access to a role, everyone who has that role at a point gets it too. Items you chose yourself stay as they are.

| Role | For whom |
| --- | --- |
| **Cashier** | A person who sells at the cash desk |
| **Warehouse viewer** | A person who only looks at the warehouse |
| **Warehouse manager** | A person who runs the warehouse |
| **Stock counter** | A person who counts goods but does not see the stock |
| **No access** | A person who does not work at this point |
| **Custom** | You choose each item yourself |

## For the whole business

These apply to every point. Most colleagues need none of them.

| Area | What you can allow |
| --- | --- |
| Business details | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete |
| Points | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete |
| Team | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Change access |
| Prices | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Restore |
| Audit trail | View the audit trail |
| What you owe suppliers | Open details |
| Payments to suppliers | Add, Cancel |

::note
**View the audit trail** gives access to the record of sensitive actions in the business, for example who reversed or deleted a goods document, and when. The owner of the business has it. It is not part of any role, so give it only to a person you trust with this record. You read it in the **Activity log** and in **History** on a record. See [See who changed what](/en/team/activity-log).
::

**What you owe suppliers** and **Payments to suppliers** let a person see what the business owes its suppliers, record payments and reverse them. Only the owner has them at first; no role includes them. See [Pay your suppliers](/en/warehouse/pay-suppliers#who-can-do-what).

## At each point: Warehouse

| Area | What you can allow |
| --- | --- |
| Warehouse | Open details |
| Products | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Restore |
| Categories | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Restore |
| Suppliers | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Restore |
| Goods received | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Receive, Cancel, Restore |
| Write-offs | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Write off, Cancel, Restore |
| Transfers | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Send, Receive, Cancel, Restore |
| Stock levels | See the list, Open details |
| Stock counts | See the list, Open details, Add, Edit, Delete, Restore, Count, Check and approve, Update stock, Cancel |

::note
For **Stock counts**, **Cancel** lets a person undo a count that already updated the stock (**Undo this count**). No role includes it, so only the owner does it, unless you give it to someone yourself. Cancelling a count before it changes the stock needs **Edit**. The **Stock counter** role can count but cannot see stock levels, deliveries or write-offs, so a count stays blind. See [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count#blind-counting).
::

The products with variants (see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-products)) have two accesses: **See products** and **Manage products**. **See products** is in the roles **Cashier**, **Warehouse viewer**, **Warehouse manager** and **Stock counter**. **Manage products** is only in **Warehouse manager**. **Manage products** lets a person add, change, link, archive and restore products and receive stock from them. Persons who could already add, change or remove items got the matching access automatically.

## At each point: Cash desk

| Area | What you can allow |
| --- | --- |
| Products at the till | Open details |
| Sales | Add, Open details, Refund, See costs and profit |
| Shifts | Start, Open details, Close, See every cashier's shift, Close someone else's shift |
| Cash in and out of the drawer | Record, Undo |
| Daily cash report | See |
| Cashier PINs | Manage |
| Receipt settings | Open details, Edit |
| Activity log of this point | See the activity log of this point |

::note
Without **See every cashier's shift**, a cashier opens only their own shift. The shifts of colleagues are hidden from them. Owners and point admins always see every shift. If a manager has a **Custom** role, tick **See every cashier's shift** for them so they can see the shift totals of the whole point and the shifts that are still open.
::

The **Cashier** role includes **Record** for **Cash in and out of the drawer**: a cashier can put cash into their own drawer and take it out. **Undo**, **Close someone else's shift**, **See every cashier's shift** and the **Daily cash report** are in no role, so only the owner has them at first. Give them one by one to the people who check the cash, for example a manager. To give **Undo**, see [Undo an entry made by mistake](/en/cashier/cash-in-and-out#undo). See [Put cash in or take cash out](/en/cashier/cash-in-and-out), [Manage open shifts](/en/reports/open-shifts) and [Read the daily cash report](/en/reports/cash-report).

Point admins have **See the activity log of this point** automatically. For anyone else, tick it for each point. The log of the whole business, with entries that belong to no point, is for the owner only. See [See who changed what](/en/team/activity-log#who-can-see).

::tip
**See costs and profit** is the access that shows costs and profit. Give it only to people who may know your margins. Without it, the cash desk and the price pages hide the cost and the profit.
::
