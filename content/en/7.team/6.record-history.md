**History** shows everything that happened to one record: who created it, who changed it and when. It is the quick way to answer "who did this?" without searching the whole Activity log.

## Open the history of a record

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the record

Open the record, for example a goods receipt or an item.

### Press History

Press **History** under the page title. A panel opens on the side. On a phone it opens from the bottom.

### Read the entries

The newest entry is first. Each entry says what was done, who did it and when. If an action was refused or failed, you see that too.

### Show the details

Press **Show changes** to see **Field**, **Before** and **After**. Press **Hide changes** to close them.

### Close the panel

Press **Close**.
::

The panel shows the last 12 months only. If you do not see the **History** button, you do not have access to the log. See [who can see what](/en/team/activity-log#who-can-see).

[]{#which-records}

## Which records have History

- **Warehouse documents:** goods receipts, write-offs, transfers and stock counts.
- **Warehouse lists:** items, categories and suppliers.
- **Prices.**
- **Supplier debts and payments.**
- **Cash desk:** sales, refunds, cash in and out of the drawer, and shifts, in the back office.
- **Business settings:** business details, tax, receipt, cost method and stock-count rules, on the business settings page.
- **Point settings:** point details, apps and till settings, on the point settings page.
- **A staff member:** access changes, the invitations the person joined through, and when they were added.

::note
On a staff member, **History** shows access changes, invitations and additions only. Removing a person is not recorded yet.
::
