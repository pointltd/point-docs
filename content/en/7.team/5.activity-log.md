The **Activity log** shows who changed what and when. Use it to check who changed a price, posted a stock document, changed a setting or gave someone access. Refused attempts, such as a wrong PIN at the till, are kept too.

::note
The Activity log is in the back office only, not in the Windows till. Entries are kept for 12 months. Changes made before this screen was added are not in the log.
::

## Open the Activity log

::steps{level="3"}
### Open your business

Sign in to the back office and open your business.

### Press Activity log

In the business menu, press **Activity log**. You see the newest entries first.

### Read the entries

Each row shows when it happened, who did it, what they did and the outcome. Press **Show changes** to see the details.
::

If you do not see **Activity log** in the menu, you do not have access yet. See [who can see what](#who-can-see).

[]{#filters}

## Find an entry with filters

The log opens with the last 7 days. Press the filters button to narrow it down. Press **Apply** to use your choice.

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **From** and **To** | The days to look at. Each day is a whole day in the time zone of the :term[point]{id="point"}. The label shows the zone, for example "From (Dushanbe)". You can look back 12 months. |
| Person | Only the actions of one colleague. Type a name to search. |
| Record type | Only one kind of record, for example goods receipts or prices. The types are grouped: Warehouse, Prices, Cashier, Payables, Team and Settings. |
| Action | One action, for example posting. You can pick it after you choose a record type. |
| Outcome | **Done**, **Refused** or **Failed**. By default you see all three. |
| Filter by point | Only one point. Only the owner sees this filter. |

Each filter you use shows as a small tag you can remove. Press **Clear filters** to start again.

## Read an entry

- **When:** the date and time, with the time zone of the point.
- **Who:** the person. If a cashier did it at the till, you see "Anna on Till 1". Work done by the app shows as **Partha (automatic)**. A person who left shows the tag **no longer on staff**.
- **What:** a short sentence. The name of a record is a link when it still exists and you may open it. A deleted record shows as "deleted" with its type.
- **Outcome:** **Done**, **Refused** or **Failed**. Refused and failed entries have a red mark at the start of the row.

Press **Show changes** to see a table with **Field**, **Before** and **After**. Names in the table are today's names, so a renamed item shows its new name.

The owner also sees the IP address and the device of each entry.

To see more, press **Load older entries** at the bottom. The log shows 25 entries at a time.

[]{#who-can-see}

## Who can see what

- **The owner** sees everything in the business, with the IP address and the device.
- **Admins of a point** see the entries of their own point automatically. A notice says "You see entries for your point only."
- **Other colleagues** see the entries of a point only when the owner ticks **See the activity log of this point** for them. Do it in **Team**, in the access of the person. See [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).
- **Entries that belong to no point**, such as business settings and staff access, are for the owner only.

::tip
To see the log of a single record, such as one goods receipt, open the record and press **History**. See [See the history of a record](/en/team/record-history).
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something does not work

- **There is no Activity log in the menu.** Ask the owner to tick **See the activity log of this point** for you.
- **You see "No entries found".** Choose a wider date range or press **Clear filters**.
- **A name is missing or shows an old value.** Try again a little later.
- **You see "We could not load the activity log."** Press **Try again**. If it keeps failing, write to support and send the request number shown on the screen.
