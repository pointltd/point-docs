You can add the people who work with you: a cashier, a stock keeper, a manager. Each person gets only the access you give them. A cashier does not need to see your prices, and a stock keeper does not need your cash desk.

::note
You need access to **Team** to do this. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see it. Add your :term[points]{id="point"} first, because you give access point by point: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point).
::

## Add a person

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Team

Open your business, then **Team**. You see the people who work with you. If nobody else is here yet, it says "You are the only one here."

### Press Add a person

Press **Add a person**. A form opens.

### Type their name

Type the **First name**. The **Last name** is optional. You do not need an email: a person who works at the till only needs a name.

### Choose what they can do

Under **By point**, each of your points has a **Role** list. Choose the one that fits:

- **Cashier**: sells at the cash desk.
- **Warehouse viewer**: can look at the warehouse, but not change it.
- **Warehouse manager**: works with the warehouse.
- **No access**: this person does not work at this point.
- **Custom**: you tick exactly what they can do.

Under each point you see how many actions the person is allowed, for example "12 of 84 actions allowed". To see and change them one by one, press **Customize**. Press **Hide details** to fold the list again. If you change a single item, the role becomes **Custom**. See [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).

Under **For the whole business**, tick what applies to every point, for example **Prices**. Most people need nothing here.

:::screenshot{src="team/invite-staff-2" alt="Choosing the Cashier role for a person at a point"}
:::

### Press Add person

Press **Add person**. A window opens with **Code for** and the person's name, and a number of several digits. Give it to them now. It works once, for 24 hours.
::

::warning
Give access to at least one point. Without it the app says "Give access to at least one point."
::

## Give them the code

The window shows three steps for the person. Read them out or print the page:

1. At the till, tap your name.
2. Type this code.
3. Then choose your own PIN.

Press **Copy** to copy the code or **Print** to print a sheet. The code is shown only now. Write it down or print it before you press **Done**. If you lose it, you can always give a new code: see [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).

If the window says "The person is added, but there is no code yet", the person is on the team, but the app could not make a code just now. Open **Team** and press **Give a new code** next to their name.

## Let them sign in online

If the person also needs to work in Partha on a computer or phone, not only at the till, press **Let them sign in online (email)** under the form. Type their email, choose their roles and press **Invite**. They get a message with a link. You see "Employee invited successfully!"

## What the person does with the link

The link only shows the invitation. Nobody joins until they press the button, so a mail program that opens links to check them cannot add anyone by mistake.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the link

The page says who invited them and to what, for example "You have been invited to “Fruit Shop” as Cashier". It shows the email the invitation was sent to with the middle hidden, for example "s***e@gmail.com".

### Sign in or create an account

If they are not signed in, they press **Sign in** or **Create an account**, using the email the invitation was sent to. Then they come back to the same page.

### Press Accept invitation

They press **Accept invitation** and land on their home page, ready to work.
::

If the page says "This invitation was sent to a different email address", the person is signed in with another account. They press **Sign in with another account** and use the one the invitation was sent to. If it says the invitation was cancelled, has expired or was already accepted, ask the owner of the business to send a new one.


## See and cancel invitations

People you invited by email and who have not joined yet are listed under **Waiting for an answer**, at the bottom of **Team**. For each one you see the email and "Link works until" a date. The link works for 7 days.

If you sent an invitation by mistake, press **Cancel invitation** next to it and confirm. The link in their email stops working. You can invite the same person again later. Only people who may add people to the team see the button.

On the home page of your point, the step **Invite a colleague** is ticked as soon as you have invited someone.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

When the person opens the link and sees a message:

- **"This invitation was cancelled or the link is not valid."** The owner cancelled it, or the link was not copied in full. Ask the owner of the business to send a new invitation.
- **"This invitation has expired."** The link works for 7 days. Ask the owner to send a new one.
- **"This invitation was already accepted by someone else."** An invitation works for one person only. Ask the owner to send a new one.
- **"This invitation was already accepted."** If it was you, go to the home page. If not, ask the owner to send a new one.
- **"You already work in this business."** You are already on the team. Go to the home page.
- **"This invitation was sent to a different email address."** Sign in with the account that received the invitation.
- **"We could not accept the invitation."** Try again in a few minutes.

When you add a person:

- **"Give access to at least one point."** Choose a role for at least one point.
- **"Add a point first, then you can give access to it."** You have no points yet: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point).
- **"We could not add this person. Try again."** Check the name and try again.
- **"Error while inviting employee!"** Check the email and try again.
- **You do not see Team or Add a person.** You do not have access. Ask the owner of the business.
