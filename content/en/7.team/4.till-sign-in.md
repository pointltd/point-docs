At the Windows till, everyone signs in with their own PIN. The till does not ask for a password, only for a name and a PIN. This page shows what you do as the owner of the business, and what you do so your team can sell too.

::note
You need access to **Team** and to **Cashier PINs** to do this. The owner of the business has both. Someone else needs you to give them these two: see [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions).
::

## Who is on the "Who is selling?" list

The till shows a name only when all of this is true for the :term[point]{id="point"} where the till stands:

- The person is on your **Team**.
- The person may sell at this point. For a colleague, this means the **Cashier** role for the point. You, as the owner, may do everything at every point, so you need nothing extra.
- The person has a PIN, or a setup code that is not used and not expired.
- The **Cash desk** is switched on at the point: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point#apps).

## Sign in at the till as the owner

You are already on your **Team**, because Partha added you when you created the business. You do not give yourself a role. You only need a setup code, once.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Team
In Partha, open your business, then **Team**. Find your own name in the list.

### Give yourself a code
Next to your name, press the ticket icon (**Give a new code**). A window shows your code. It works once, for 24 hours. Keep the window open, or write the code down.

### Choose your PIN at the till
Go to the till. The screen **Who is selling?** checks the list every few seconds, so your name appears by itself. Press your name, type the code and choose a PIN of 4 to 8 digits. Type the same PIN again. The till opens the cash desk.
::

The next time, press your name and type your PIN. If you forget it, go to **Team**, press the open-lock icon (**Remove PIN**) next to your name and confirm. Then press **Give a new code** and choose a new PIN at the till.

::tip
Choose a PIN that others cannot guess, and do not share it. Your PIN is what shows that it was you at the till.
::

## Let your team sell at the till

Do this once for each person.

::steps{level="3"}
### Give them access to the point
If the person is new, press **Add a person**, type their name and under **By point** choose the **Cashier** role for the point where the till stands. See [Add a person to your team](/en/team/invite-staff).

If the person is already on your team, press the key icon (**Change what they can do**) next to their name, choose the **Cashier** role for the point and press **Update Permissions**. See [Change what a colleague can do](/en/team/change-access).

### Give them a code
Press **Give a new code** next to their name, or copy the code that the window shows right after you add them. Give the code to the person. It works once, for 24 hours.

### They choose a PIN at the till
The person presses their name on the **Who is selling?** screen, types the code and chooses their own PIN. You never see or choose their PIN.
::

In **Team**, the column **Till PIN** shows where each person is: **No PIN**, **Waiting for setup** (they have a code, but have not chosen a PIN yet) or **PIN set**.

## If something went wrong

- **A name is missing from the list.** Check the four points under "Who is on the list" above. The most common reason is a missing **Cashier** role for this point, or a code that is older than 24 hours. Press **Give a new code**.
- **The till says "Nobody can sign in at this till yet".** No one on your team has a code or a PIN for this point. Follow the steps above.
- **The till says "The setup code is wrong or has expired".** Press **Give a new code** next to their name and give them the new code.
- **A person forgot their PIN.** Press **Remove PIN** next to their name, confirm, then press **Give a new code**.
- **You do not see the ticket icon.** You do not have the access **Cashier PINs**. Ask the owner of the business.
