People change jobs. Here you change what a colleague can do, give them a PIN for a shared till, or stop their access.

::note
You need access to change **Team** and its access. Ask the owner of the business if you do not see the buttons.
::

## Change what a colleague can do

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the person

Open your business, then **Team**. Next to the person, press the key icon (**Change what they can do**).

### Change the roles

For each point, choose a **Role** or tick single items. The change applies to every point of the business: it says "Applies to all your points" on the page.

### Press Update Permissions

Press **Update Permissions**. If it does not save, the app says "Could not save. Please try again."
::

## Change a name, email or phone

Next to the person, press the pencil icon (**Edit employee**). Change **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email** or **Phone** and press **Update Employee**. You see "Employee updated successfully!"

## Give a new code for the till

On a shared till, a cashier starts work by tapping their name and typing their own PIN. You do not choose the PIN. You give a one-time code, and the person chooses their PIN with it.

Open **Team**. Next to the person you see **Till PIN** with one of these: **PIN set**, **Waiting for setup** or **No PIN**. Press the ticket icon (**Give a new code**). A window shows the code for the person. It works once, for 24 hours, and it is shown only now: write it down or print it. The person taps their name at the till, types the code and chooses a new PIN.

To take a PIN away, for example when a person leaves, press the open-lock icon (**Remove PIN**) and confirm. The person cannot start work at the till until you give a new code. A cashier can also change their own PIN: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).

:::screenshot{src="team/change-access-1" alt="The one-time code a new cashier types at the till"}
:::

## Stop someone's access

The app has no button that deletes a colleague. To stop access, open **Change what they can do**, set **No access** for every point, clear **For the whole business**, and press **Update Permissions**.

::caution
Do this at once when someone leaves, so they cannot use the till or see your numbers.
::
