At the end of your work, you close the shift. You count the cash in the drawer and Partha compares it with the cash that should be there. If anything is missing or extra, you see it at once.

::note
You close your own shift. A manager with the access can also close it for you in the back office: see [Close a cashier's shift](/en/reports/open-shifts#close-someone-elses-shift). After a shift is closed, no more sales can be added to it. To keep selling, open a new shift: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
::

## Close a shift

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the Shift screen

Open the cash desk and press **Shift** in the menu. You see the shift that is open now.

### Look at the shift so far

The screen shows when the shift was **Opened**, and the cash of the drawer line by line:

- **Cash at the start**: what you typed when you opened the shift.
- **Cash sales**: the cash customers paid, minus the change you gave back.
- **Cash refunds**: the cash you gave back for refunds.
- One line for each cash in, pay out and cash drop you recorded: see [Put cash in or take cash out](/en/cashier/cash-in-and-out).
- **Should be in the drawer**: what the lines above add up to. Partha works this out for you.

Below it are the numbers of the shift: **Sales**, how much was **Paid in cash** and **Paid by card**, **Discounts given**, **Refunds**, and **Taken in total**.

### Count the cash

Count the money in the drawer. Type it in **Cash you counted**. Partha shows the result at once:

- **The cash matches**: all is well.
- **Cash is missing**: you have less than you should. The amount is shown.
- **There is extra cash**: you have more than you should. The amount is shown.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/close-shift-1" alt="The Shift screen with the counted cash and Close shift"}
:::

### Add a note (optional)

In **Note (optional)** you can write why, for example "gave change from my own pocket". This helps the owner later.

### Press Close shift

Press **Close shift**. Partha asks **Close this shift?** Press **Close shift** again to confirm.
::

::caution
You cannot reopen a closed shift. If you close it by mistake, open a new shift to keep selling.
::

## What you see after closing

The screen **Shift closed** shows the same lines of the drawer, then **Counted**: the cash you typed, and the result: **Over**, **Short** or **No difference**. These numbers are final. Press **Open a new shift** when you start again.

[]{#over-or-short}

## Over and short

:term[Over and short]{id="over-short"} is the difference between the cash you counted and the cash that should be in the drawer:

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **No difference** | You counted exactly what should be there. |
| **Over** | There is more cash than there should be. |
| **Short** | There is less cash than there should be. |

Partha works it out like this: **Counted** minus **Should be in the drawer**. **Should be in the drawer** is **Cash at the start**, plus **Cash sales**, minus **Cash refunds**, plus each cash in, minus each pay out and cash drop.

For example: should be in the drawer 250.00, you counted 240.00. Short: 10.00.

Common reasons for a difference:

- Wrong change was given to a customer.
- Cash was put in or taken out, but not recorded: see [Put cash in or take cash out](/en/cashier/cash-in-and-out).
- A sale paid by card was recorded as paid in cash, or the other way round.
- The cash was counted or typed wrong.

A cash entry can be undone only while the shift is open. After the close, a mistake stays in the over or short of that shift, so the count is never rewritten. Write what happened in **Note (optional)**: the owner sees it in the back office.

[]{#closed-in-back-office}

## Your shift was closed in the back office

A manager can close your shift in the back office, for example when you went home without closing it. They count the drawer and write why.

On the **Windows till**, you then see at the top of the screen: **"This shift is already closed on the server (in the back office, or by the till itself). Count the drawer, close the shift here, then open a new one before you sell again."** Press **Close the shift**, count the drawer and close the shift on the till as usual, then open a new shift. Sales you made on the till before closing it there are kept, and they show in [Problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales) for the owner to check.

When you sell **in the browser**, a sale on that shift is refused with **"This shift is already closed. Open a new one to keep selling."** Open a new shift.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **Cash is missing or extra.** Count the money again. Check that you typed the amount right. Check the sales and the refunds on the shift. If the difference stays, write the reason in **Note (optional)** and tell the owner.
- **"Only the cashier who opened a shift can close it."** Ask the cashier who opened it to close it, or a manager who can close it in the back office.
- **"This shift is already closed. Open a new one to keep selling."** Somebody has closed it. Open a new shift.
- **"No connection to the server. Check the internet and try again."** Check the internet and press **Try again**.
- **The Close shift button does not work.** Type the cash you counted first. Type 0 if the drawer is empty.
