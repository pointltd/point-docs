At the end of your work, you close the shift. You count the cash in the drawer and Partha compares it with the cash that should be there. If anything is missing or extra, you see it at once.

::note
Only the cashier who opened a shift can close it. After you close it, no more sales can be added to it. To keep selling, open a new shift: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
::

## Close a shift

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the Shift screen

Open the cash desk and press **Shift** in the menu. You see the shift that is open now.

### Look at the shift so far

The screen shows when the shift was **Opened**, and the money in the drawer:

- **Cash in the drawer at the start**: what you typed when you opened the shift.
- **Cash from sales, minus cash given back**: the cash you took and the change and refunds you paid.
- **Cash that should be in the drawer**: the sum of the two. Partha works this out for you.

Below it are the numbers of the shift: **Sales**, how much was **Paid in cash** and **Paid by card**, **Discounts given**, **Refunds**, and **Taken in total**.

### Count the cash

Count the money in the drawer. Type it in **Cash you counted**. Partha shows the result at once:

- **The cash matches**: all is well.
- **Cash is missing**: you have less than you should. The amount is shown.
- **There is extra cash**: you have more than you should. The amount is shown.

### Add a note (optional)

In **Note (optional)** you can write why, for example "gave change from my own pocket". This helps the owner later.

### Press Close shift

Press **Close shift**. Partha asks **Close this shift?** Press **Close shift** again to confirm.
::

::caution
You cannot reopen a closed shift. If you close it by mistake, open a new shift to keep selling.
::

## What you see after closing

The screen **Shift closed** shows three lines: **Cash that should be in the drawer**, **Cash you counted** and the difference. These numbers are final. Press **Open a new shift** when you start again.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **Cash is missing or extra.** Count the money again. Check that you typed the amount right. Check the sales and the refunds on the shift. If the difference stays, write the reason in **Note (optional)** and tell the owner.
- **"Only the cashier who opened a shift can close it."** Ask the cashier who opened it to close it.
- **"This shift is already closed. Open a new one to keep selling."** Somebody has closed it. Open a new shift.
- **"No connection to the server. Check the internet and try again."** Check the internet and press **Try again**.
- **The Close shift button does not work.** Type the cash you counted first. Type 0 if the drawer is empty.
