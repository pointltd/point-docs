A sale is the list of products a customer buys. You build it at the cash desk, tap by tap or by scanning, and then press **Pay**.

::note
You need an open shift to sell: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift). Without one, the cash desk shows **Open a shift to start selling**.
::

## Build a sale

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the cash desk

Open your :term[point]{id="point"}, then **Cash desk**. The products are on the left. On a phone, the sale is hidden: press **Open the sale** to see it.

### Add products

Do any of these. They all work together in one sale.

- **Tap a product.** Use the tabs **Favourites**, **All** or a category to find it.
- **Scan the barcode.** The product is added. Scan it again to add one more.
- **Search.** Type a name or a barcode in **Scan or search a product**. If only one product matches, press :kbd{value="Enter"} to add it.

### Change the amounts

Each product is a row in **Current sale**. Press **+** for one more and **−** for one less. When only one is left, **−** removes the row.

### Add a discount (optional)

Press **Discount** to take something off the whole sale. Choose **Percent** or **Amount**, type the number and press **Apply discount**. The total shows **Before discount**, the **Discount** and the **Total**.

### Press Pay

When the sale is right, press **Pay** with the total on it. See [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment).
::

::tip
Pick the products you sell most as favourites. On the **Favourites** tab, press **Pick favourites**, tap those products and press **Done**. They are saved on this device.
::

## Sell something that is not in your products

Press **Quick item**, type a price, and add a name if you like. Press **Add to sale**. Use this for one-time goods, or for a product that has no price yet. A product with no price asks for the price when you tap it.

## Serve two customers at once

Press **Hold sale**. It is put aside on this device, and you can serve the next customer. To come back, press **Held** and then **Recall** next to that sale. **Throw this sale away** deletes it.

To empty the sale, press **Clear** and confirm. This cannot be undone.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"Barcode not found."** No product has this barcode. Press **Sell as quick item** to sell it anyway, or add the product in the **Warehouse** (see [Add products](/en/warehouse/add-items)).
- **"Nothing found."** Try a shorter name, or part of the barcode.
- **"No products yet."** Add products first, or use **Quick item**.
- **"Could not load the products."** Press **Try again**.
- **"No internet connection."** You can keep building the sale, but it cannot be finished until the connection is back.
- **"Open a shift first."** Open a shift, then come back.
