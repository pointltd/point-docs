A :term[shift]{id="shift"} is your working time at the till. You open it at the start of the day with the cash that is in the drawer. Partha counts your sales from that moment, and at the end tells you how much cash should be there.

::note
You cannot sell without an open shift. The **Cash desk** is only for :term[points]{id="point"} that sell. If you do not see it, ask the owner of the business: see [Add a point](/en/business/add-point#apps).
::

## Open a shift

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the cash desk

Open your point, then **Cash desk**. If no shift is open, you see **Open a shift to start selling**. Press **Open a shift**. You can also press **Shift** in the menu.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/open-shift-1" alt="The cash desk before a shift is open, with the Open a shift button"}
:::

### Count the cash in the drawer

Count the money that is in the drawer now, before the first sale. You will type this amount.

### Check the currency

**Currency of this shift** is filled in for you. All sales in this shift use this currency. Change it only if you sell in another one.

### Type the cash you have

In **Cash in the drawer at the start**, type the amount you counted. If the drawer is empty, leave 0.

### Press Open shift

Press **Open shift**. The sale screen opens, and the top of the screen says **Shift open**. You can start selling.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/open-shift-2" alt="The Open a shift screen with the starting cash typed in"}
:::
::

::tip
On a shared till, set your PIN. At the bottom of the **Shift** screen, open **My PIN** and choose 4 to 8 digits. Then you start work by tapping your name.
::

## If something went wrong

- **"Open a shift first. A sale needs an open shift."** You tried to sell before opening a shift. Press **Open a shift**.
- **"No connection to the server. Check the internet and try again."** The till could not reach the server, and no shift was opened. Check the internet and press **Open shift** again.
- **"You already have an open shift at this point."** You do not need a new one. Go to the sale screen and keep selling. To start a new shift, close the old one first.
- **"The currency does not match your open shift. Sell in the currency of the shift, or close it and open a new one."** The sale is in a different currency from your shift. Sell in the currency of the shift, or close the shift and open a new one.
- **You cannot find the Cash desk.** The point may not sell, or you may not have access. Ask the owner of the business.
