The Partha Till app on Windows keeps selling when the internet is down. It saves every sale on the computer and sends them to Partha as soon as the connection is back. Nothing is lost and you do not have to do anything.

::note
This is for the Windows till. Selling in the browser needs the internet.
::

## What the cashier sees

- **No connection** at the top of the till: the internet is down. Keep selling as usual: add the products, take the payment and print the receipt.
- **Receipts print as usual.** Each receipt has its own number, also without internet.
- **Sales not sent yet.** The till shows how many sales wait to be sent. The number goes down by itself when the internet is back.
- **A warning when the limit comes close.** A till may sell without internet for a limited time, usually 7 days. The till warns you halfway and again near the end. When the time is up, it stops selling until it is online again. Get the internet back before that.

::tip
A card payment without internet works only with a card machine that works on its own (it prints its own slip). Connected card machines and QR payments need the internet.
::

## What the owner sees

Open your point, then **Cash desk** → **Tills**. For each till you see:

- **Last sync**: when the till last reached Partha. A working till with internet checks in every few minutes.
- **Sales not sent yet**: the sales the till still holds, and the time of the oldest one.

Sales a till sent late appear in [Sales](/en/reports/sales) and in [Profit](/en/reports/profit) on the day they belong to. A sale shows both times: when it was sold and when Partha received it.

## What Partha checks

Partha never refuses a sale that a till made. It adds the sale, and if something about it needs a look, for example a price that was not the price of that day, or more goods sold than were in stock, it lists the sale under **Cash desk** → **Problem sales**. See [Check problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales).

## If something went wrong

- **The till says how many sales are not sent, and the number does not go down.** Check the internet on the till's computer. If the internet works and the number still stays, see [Problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales) and call support.
- **The till stopped selling and asks for the internet.** The time to sell without internet is up. Connect the computer to the internet. The till sends the sales and starts selling again.
- **The till says "Sales cannot be sent. Call support."** Some sales of this till are stuck. Keep the computer as it is, do not remove the till or delete anything, and call support.
- **The till says it is not found.** See [Troubleshooting](/en/help/troubleshooting#till-not-found).
