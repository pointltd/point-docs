The Partha Till app on Windows keeps selling when the internet is down. It saves every sale on the computer and sends them to Partha as soon as the connection is back. Nothing is lost and you do not have to do anything.

::note
This is for the Windows till. Selling in the browser needs the internet.
::

## What the cashier sees

- **No connection** at the top of the till: the internet is down. Keep selling as usual: add the products, take the payment and print the receipt.
- **Receipts print as usual.** Each receipt has its own number, also without internet.
- **N to send** next to **No connection** (for example “3 to send”). It is the number of sales that wait to be sent. It goes down by itself when the internet is back.
- **A warning when the limit comes close.** A till may sell without internet for a limited time, usually 7 days. The till warns you halfway (“No connection for a while: the till stops selling in N h. Connect it to the internet.”) and again near the end (“No connection for a long time: …”). When the time is up, it stops selling: “This till has been offline for too long and has stopped selling.” You can still close your shift and reprint receipts. Connect the till to the internet: the sales are sent and selling starts again. Get the internet back before that.

::tip
A card payment without internet works only with a card machine that works on its own (it prints its own slip).
::

## Good to know

- **A new till needs the internet once** to load the products and prices. Until then it says “The till has no catalog yet. Connect to the internet once so it can load the products.”
- **Each cashier signs in once while the till is online.** After that the PIN also works without internet. Before that, the lock screen says “This person cannot sign in without the internet yet.”
- **After the time limit, signing in is limited.** A cashier who signs in then can only close the shift and reprint receipts: “Once the till is back online, sign in again to sell.”
- **Refunds.** A sale made on this till can be refunded without internet, with a manager's PIN where your business requires it. A sale from another till can be refunded only when the till is online and you are signed in online.
- **Wrong computer clock.** If the computer's clock is earlier than the last sale, the till cannot sell. A manager sets the right time with **Set the time** on the lock screen.

## What the owner sees

Open your point, then **Cash desk** → **Tills**. For each till you see:

- **Last sync**: when the till last reached Partha. A working till with internet checks in every few minutes.
- **Sales not sent yet: N, the oldest from …**: the sales the till still holds, and the time of the oldest one.

Sales a till sent late appear in [Sales](/en/reports/sales) and in [Profit](/en/reports/profit) on the day they belong to. A sale shows both times (**Sold** and **Received by Partha**) and the day it is counted on (**Counted on**).

## What Partha checks

Partha never refuses a sale that a till made. It adds the sale, and if something about it needs a look, for example a price that was not the price of that day, or more goods sold than were in stock, it lists the sale under **Cash desk** → **Problem sales**. See [Check problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales).

## If something went wrong

- **The till says how many sales are not sent, and the number does not go down.** Check the internet on the till's computer. If the internet works and the number still stays, see [Problem sales](/en/cashier/problem-sales) and call support.
- **The till stopped selling and asks for the internet.** The time to sell without internet is up. Connect the computer to the internet. The till sends the sales and starts selling again.
- **The till says its sales cannot be sent and asks you to call support.** Some sales of this till are stuck. Keep the computer as it is, do not remove the till or delete anything, and call support.
- **The till says “This till was removed or cannot reach its account. N sales are not sent. Call the owner.”** The till keeps its sales but does not sell. See [Troubleshooting](/en/help/troubleshooting#till-not-found).
- **The till says “This till is closed. Pair it again to use this computer.”** The owner closed it out. See [Remove or replace a till](/en/cashier/remove-or-replace-a-till).
- **The till says its data was copied to another computer.** This copy stopped selling. Call support.
