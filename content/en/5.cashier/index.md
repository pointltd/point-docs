Everything you do at the till, in the order of a working day.

::card-group
  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-monitor-down
  title: Install the Partha Till app on Windows
  to: /en/cashier/install-till-app
  ---
  Download the Partha Till app, install it on the point's Windows computer and connect it to your business.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-key-round
  title: Open a shift
  to: /en/cashier/open-shift
  ---
  Count the cash in the drawer and open a shift, so you can start selling.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-shopping-cart
  title: Make a sale
  to: /en/cashier/make-sale
  ---
  Add products to a sale at the cash desk, change the amounts and give a discount, then go to payment.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-banknote
  title: Take payment
  to: /en/cashier/take-payment
  ---
  Take cash or a card payment, see the change to give back and finish the sale.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-receipt
  title: Receipts
  to: /en/cashier/receipts
  ---
  Print a receipt, and find today's receipts at the cash desk.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-undo-2
  title: Refund a sale
  to: /en/cashier/refund
  ---
  Return the money for a whole receipt, and put the products back in stock.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-arrow-left-right
  title: Put cash in or take cash out
  to: /en/cashier/cash-in-and-out
  ---
  Record cash you put into the drawer or take out of it, and undo an entry made by mistake.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-lock
  title: Close a shift
  to: /en/cashier/close-shift
  ---
  Count the cash at the end of the day, see if anything is missing and close the shift.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-wifi-off
  title: Sell without internet
  to: /en/cashier/sell-without-internet
  ---
  The Windows till keeps selling when the internet is down, and sends the sales to Partha when it is back.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-circle-alert
  title: Check problem sales
  to: /en/cashier/problem-sales
  ---
  See the sales from the tills that Partha added but that need a look, and mark them resolved.
  ::

  ::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-replace
  title: Remove or replace a till
  to: /en/cashier/remove-or-replace-a-till
  ---
  Remove a till without losing its sales, close it out, and add the same computer again as a new till.
  ::
