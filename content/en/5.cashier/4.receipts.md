A receipt is the record of one sale. When you finish a sale, Partha opens its receipt. You can print it, and you can open any receipt from today again later.

::note
You need the **Cash desk** to do this. A receipt is made when you finish a sale: see [Take payment](/en/cashier/take-payment).
::

## Print a receipt

::steps{level="3"}
### Finish the sale

When you press **Finish sale**, the receipt opens. On it you see the name of your :term[point]{id="point"}, the receipt number, the time, the cashier, each product with its amount and price, the **Discount** if there was one, and the total. At the end is a barcode of the receipt number, so the receipt can be scanned later.

### Choose the paper width

Under **Paper width**, press **58 mm** or **80 mm**. Choose the width of the paper in your printer.

### Press Print receipt

Press **Print receipt**. Your browser opens its print window. Choose your printer and print.

:::
::

::note
**Send to customer** is not available yet. The app says "Sending receipts is not available yet."
::

## Start the next sale

On the receipt screen, press **New sale** to go back to the cash desk.

## Find today's receipts

Open the menu of the cash desk, then **Today's receipts**. You see every receipt from today, the newest first. Each one shows:

- the receipt number, for example **Receipt 12**;
- the time, the cashier and how it was paid: **Cash** or **Card**;
- the amount;
- **Refunded**, if the money was returned (the amount is crossed out).

Press a receipt to open it again. You can print it again from there. Press **Refresh** to see the newest ones. If there are many, the list says "Showing the latest … of … receipts."

If you may see costs, a receipt also shows how much you **Earned** on it.

Typing a number under **Find a receipt** narrows the list to the receipts whose number contains it. To open a receipt from another till of this :term[point]{id="point"}, or from an earlier day, type its whole number and press **Find**. See [Refund a sale](/en/cashier/refund).

::tip
Have a barcode scanner? Scan the barcode at the end of the receipt while **Today's receipts** is open. The number goes into **Find a receipt** and the receipt opens; you do not need to press the field first. On the sale screen, a scanned receipt is not added as a product: Partha says **This is a receipt's barcode** and offers **Find this receipt**.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"No receipts yet today."** Receipts appear here as soon as a sale is finished.
- **"No receipt chosen."** Open the receipt from **Today's receipts**.
- **The print window does not open.** Check that your browser allows printing, and that your printer is connected. Then press **Print receipt** again.
- **A receipt from an earlier day, or from another till.** Type its number under **Find a receipt** and press **Find**, or scan the barcode at the end of the receipt.
- **The scanner does not open the receipt.** Scan the barcode at the very end of the receipt, not the QR code. Most scanners press Enter after the code; if yours does not, press **Find** after the scan.
- **Odd signs at the end of the receipt instead of a barcode.** This receipt printer cannot print barcodes. On the Windows till, open **Receipt printer**, under **Barcode of the receipt number** press **Don't print**, and press **Save**. Receipts then print without it; type the number under **Find a receipt** instead.
