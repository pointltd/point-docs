If a customer brings the goods back, you refund the receipt. The money goes back to the customer and the products return to your stock. The receipt is not deleted: it stays in the list, marked as refunded.

::note
You need the **Cash desk** and an open shift: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift). Cash you pay back is taken from your open shift.
::

::warning
You refund the whole receipt, and only once. You cannot refund a part of it.
::

## Refund a receipt

::steps{level="3"}
### Find the receipt

Open the menu of the cash desk, then **Today's receipts**. Press the receipt you want to refund. See [Receipts](/en/cashier/receipts).

:::screenshot{src="cashier/receipts-2" alt="Today's receipts: press the receipt to refund"}
:::

Is the receipt not in the list? It may be from another till of this :term[point]{id="point"}, or from an earlier day. Under **Find a receipt**, type the number printed on the receipt, exactly as it is printed, and press **Find**. With a barcode scanner, just scan the barcode at the end of the receipt. Partha finds the receipts of every till of this point, on any day, and opens the receipt. A receipt from another till needs the internet.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/refund-1" alt="Find a receipt: type the receipt number and press Find"}
:::

### Press Refund on the receipt

On the receipt screen, press **Refund**. A window asks **Refund this receipt?**

### Write the reason (optional)

Under **Why? (optional)**, write a short reason, for example "customer changed their mind". It helps you later to remember why.

### Confirm the refund

Press **Refund**. The products go back to stock, and the receipt shows **Refunded**.

### Give the money back

If the customer paid in cash, give the cash back from the drawer. Partha takes it from your open shift. If the customer paid by card, return the money on your card terminal, because the cash desk is not connected to it.
::

## What changes after a refund

- The receipt stays in **Today's receipts** with **Refunded**, and its amount is crossed out.
- The products go back to your stock, so you can sell them again.
- When you close the shift, **Refunds** shows how many receipts were refunded and how much, and **Taken in total** is lower by that amount.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **There is no **Refund** button.** The receipt was already refunded. A refunded receipt shows **Refunded**.
- **"This receipt has already been refunded."** Somebody refunded it first. Press **Refresh** in **Today's receipts**.
- **"No connection to the server. Check the internet and try again."** The till could not reach the server, and nothing was refunded. Check the internet and press **Refund** again.
- **"Open a shift first."** Open a shift, then refund. See [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
- **"You are not allowed to do this. Ask the owner to give you access."** Your account cannot do refunds. Ask the owner of the business.
- **"This receipt was not found."** Check the number on the receipt and type it again, with its letters and dashes. **Find a receipt** finds only the receipts of this point. A receipt from another point of your business is refunded in the back office: see [See your sales](/en/reports/sales).
- **"This receipt is not on this till, and the server could not be asked: it needs a connection."** The Windows till has no internet now, and the receipt is from another till. Connect the till to the internet, sign in again if it asks, and press **Find** again.
- **"Several sales have this receipt number, so the till cannot tell which one it is."** Refund it in the back office, under **Cash desk**, **Sales**: see [See your sales](/en/reports/sales).
