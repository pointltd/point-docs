If a customer brings goods back, you refund them. You choose which products and how many. The money goes back to the customer and the products return to your stock. The receipt is not deleted: it stays in the list, marked **Partly refunded** or **Fully refunded**.

::note
You need the **Cash desk** and an open shift: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift). Cash you pay back is taken from your open shift.
::

::tip
You can refund a receipt more than once, until nothing is left on it. For example, a customer brings back 2 of 5 bottles today and the other 3 next week.
::

## Refund a receipt

::steps{level="3"}
### Find the receipt

Open the menu of the cash desk, then **Today's receipts**. Press the receipt you want to refund. See [Receipts](/en/cashier/receipts).

:::screenshot{src="cashier/receipts-2" alt="Today's receipts: press the receipt to refund"}
:::

Is the receipt not in the list? It may be from another till of this :term[point]{id="point"}, or from an earlier day. Under **Find a receipt**, type the number printed on the receipt, exactly as it is printed, and press **Find**. With a barcode scanner, just scan the barcode at the end of the receipt. Partha finds the receipts of every till of this point, on any day, and opens the receipt. A receipt from another till needs the internet.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/refund-1" alt="Find a receipt: type the receipt number and press Find"}
:::

### Press Refund on the receipt

On the receipt screen, press **Refund**. A window opens with the title **Refund receipt** and the receipt number. It lists every product of the receipt with three numbers: **Sold**, **Already refunded** and **Left**.

### Choose what comes back

For each product, set how many come back with the plus and minus buttons. You cannot refund more than is **Left**: Partha says "You can refund at most …" and stops you. To give back everything that is left, press **Refund all**.

Under **Refund total** you see the amount, and under **Returned as:** how it goes back, **cash** or **card**.

A product that was free or fully discounted can be refunded too, even on its own. The refund total is then 0, and the product still goes back to stock.

### Write the reason (optional)

Under **Why? (optional)**, write a short reason, for example "customer changed their mind". It helps you later to remember why.

### Confirm the refund

Press **Refund** with the amount, for example **Refund 25.00**. To leave without refunding, press **Close without refunding**. The products go back to stock, and the receipt shows **Partly refunded** or, when nothing is left, **Fully refunded**. The refund receipt prints at once.

### Give the money back

If the customer paid in cash, give the cash back from the drawer. Partha takes it from your open shift. If the customer paid by card, return the money on your card terminal, because the cash desk is not connected to it. The money goes back the way the sale was paid: in cash only up to what was paid in cash, the rest by card.
::

[]{#refund-receipt}

## The refund receipt

When you confirm a refund, the till prints a refund receipt for the customer: it shows the products and the amount you gave back. If the printer was busy, or you need another copy, open the sale in the back office, **Cash desk** → **Sales**, and press **Print refund receipt** next to the refund. On the till, the receipt screen has **Print again**.

[]{#manager-approval}

## When a manager must approve

Some businesses ask for a manager's approval of a refund. Then the window says **A manager must approve this refund.** On the **Windows till**, press **Approve with PIN**, and the manager types their PIN. The browser till does not have this step yet: ask a manager to do the refund in the Windows till.

[]{#offline}

## Refund without internet

The Windows till can refund a sale made on this same till without internet, and it works as above. A sale made on another till can be refunded only when the till is online. If you try it offline, the window says **This sale was made on another till. Connect to the internet to refund it.** Connect the till to the internet and try again.

[]{#sent-twice}

## A refund sent twice is made once

When the internet is slow, the till may not get an answer after you press **Refund**. Press **Refund** again with the same products and the same reason. Partha knows it is the same refund, so the customer gets the money back only once and the products go back to stock only once.

If you change the products or the reason before you try again, and the first try may have reached Partha, the window says **"This refund was already sent. Check the receipt before you refund again."** Open the receipt again and check **Already refunded** first.

## What changes after a refund

- The receipt stays in **Today's receipts** with **Partly refunded** or **Fully refunded**.
- When nothing is left, the window says **Nothing left to refund**.
- The products go back to your stock, so you can sell them again.
- When you close the shift, **Refunds** shows how many refunds were made and how much, and **Taken in total** is lower by that amount.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **There is no Refund button.** The receipt was fully refunded, or you may not refund. A fully refunded receipt shows **Fully refunded**.
- **"Nothing left to refund."** Every product of this receipt has been given back.
- **"You can refund at most … of …"** You set more than is **Left**. Lower the number.
- **"This is more than is left on the receipt. Part of it may have been refunded already. The receipt was read again: check what is left and choose again."** While the window was open, part of this receipt was refunded somewhere else, for example on another till or in the back office. Nothing was refunded now. Partha reads the receipt again and your choice starts over. Check **Left** and choose again.
- **"This refund was already sent. Check the receipt before you refund again."** The till sent this refund before, but did not get the answer, and then you changed the products or the reason. The first refund may already be made. Partha reads the receipt again and your choice starts over. Look at **Already refunded** before you refund anything more.
- **"An earlier refund of this receipt was already recorded, and what you chose now was not refunded. Check the receipt before you pay out or refund again."** An earlier try of this refund reached Partha, and then you changed what to refund. The earlier refund stands; what you chose now was not refunded. Partha reads the receipt again. Check **Already refunded** before you give any money back or refund again.
- **"This receipt has already been refunded."** Somebody refunded it first. Press **Refresh** in **Today's receipts**.
- **"Could not save the refund. Check the connection and try again."** The till could not reach the server. This message means only a connection problem. Check the internet and press **Refund** again without changing anything. If the first try did reach Partha, the refund is still made only once: see [A refund sent twice is made once](#sent-twice).
- **"A manager must approve this refund."** See [When a manager must approve](#manager-approval).
- **"This sale was made on another till. Connect to the internet to refund it."** See [Refund without internet](#offline).
- **"Open a shift first."** Open a shift, then refund. See [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
- **"You are not allowed to do this. Ask the owner to give you access."** Your account cannot do refunds. Ask the owner of the business.
- **"This receipt was not found."** Check the number on the receipt and type it again, with its letters and dashes. **Find a receipt** finds only the receipts of this point. A receipt from another point of your business is refunded in the back office: see [See your sales](/en/reports/sales).
- **"This receipt is not on this till, and the server could not be asked: it needs a connection."** The Windows till has no internet now, and the receipt is from another till. Connect the till to the internet, sign in again if it asks, and press **Find** again.
- **"Several sales have this receipt number, so the till cannot tell which one it is."** Refund it in the back office, under **Cash desk**, **Sales**: see [See your sales](/en/reports/sales).
- **In the back office, the refund did not work.** The window says why:
  - **"You have no open shift. The cash of a refund is paid out of your open shift: open a shift first, then refund."**
  - **"Your shift was closed meanwhile. Open a new shift, then refund."**
  - **"This receipt has already been refunded in full."**
  - **"This is more than is left on the receipt. Check what is left and choose again."**
  - **"A till is refunding part of this receipt right now. Wait a few minutes and check the receipt again."**
  - **"You may not refund. Ask the owner of the business."**
  - **"A manager must approve this refund with their PIN on a till."**

  **"Could not save the refund. Check the connection and try again."** here too means only a connection problem.
