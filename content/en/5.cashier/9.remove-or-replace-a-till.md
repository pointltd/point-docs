A till may hold sales it has not sent yet, for example after a day without internet. Partha keeps them safe when you remove a till or set the computer up again, as long as you follow these steps.

## Remove a till

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the Tills page

Open your point, then **Cash desk** → **Tills**.

### Press Remove

Press **Remove** next to the till. If the till still has sales that are not sent, or has not been in touch for more than 10 minutes, Partha says so first. Wait for those sales if you can: connect the till to the internet and look at **Sales not sent yet**.

### Confirm

The till stops selling right away. A removed till can still send its sales when it is back online.
::

## Close out a removed till

When the computer is gone for good, or all its sales are sent, press **Close out** next to the removed till. After that it can no longer send sales. Partha warns you if its last report had sales not sent or shifts still open.

::caution
Close out a till only when you are sure. Sales still on that computer then arrive only if you add the computer again as a till that replaces this one (below).
::

## Add the same computer again

If the till was closed out, or its key was lost, you can add the same computer again. The old till's sales stay on the computer and are sent through the new till.

::steps{level="3"}
### Add the till as usual

On **Tills**, press **Add a till**, type the code and press **Continue**. See [Install the Partha Till app on Windows](/en/cashier/install-till-app).

### Leave Replaces till ticked

If the computer still holds sales of the old till, you see **Replaces till K2 (unsent sales: 12)**, ticked. Leave it ticked: only then do those sales reach Partha. To untick it, Partha asks first; press **Keep it ticked** if you are not sure.

### Press Add this till
::

You can also set it later: press **Replaces a till…** next to the till, choose the old till and press **Save**. If a till shows **Sales of an older till on this computer that it cannot send**, do this.

## If something went wrong

- **"This computer still holds sales of a till of another point."** Add the till at the point where the computer was used before, so those sales reach the right point.
- **"Partha could not mark this till as replacing the older till."** The old till changed in the meantime. Press **Continue** to check the code again.
- **A till shows Needs attention.** Some of its sales are stuck. Call support and do not remove the till.
