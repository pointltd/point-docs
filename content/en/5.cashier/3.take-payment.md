When the sale is ready, you take the payment. Partha shows how much to take and, for cash, how much change to give back. Then you finish the sale and the receipt opens.

::note
Build the sale first: see [Make a sale](/en/cashier/make-sale). Press **Pay** to come to this screen.
::

## Take payment

::steps{level="3"}
### Check the amount

The list on the screen shows the products and the **Discount**, if there is one. **To pay** is the amount to take from the customer.

### Choose how the customer pays

Under **How is the customer paying?**, press **Cash** or **Card**.

### Cash: enter what the customer gave

Under **Cash from the customer**, press the amount the customer handed you. The buttons show common banknotes. If the customer pays the exact amount, press **Exact amount**. The sale is finished at once.

If none of the buttons fits, press **Other amount** and type the amount on the keypad.

Partha then shows **Give back**: the change for the customer. If the customer gave too little, it shows **Still to pay** in red.

### Card: charge on the terminal

Choose **Card**. The cash desk is not connected to a card terminal, so charge the full amount on your terminal first. Then continue.

### Press Finish sale

Press **Finish sale**. The button works when the customer has given enough, or you chose **Card**. Partha records the sale and opens the receipt.
::

::warning
Press **Finish sale** only after the customer has paid. For a card, wait until the terminal says the payment went through.
::

## If you need to go back

Press **Back to sale** to change the products. Nothing is recorded until you finish the sale.

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

- **"There is not enough stock of {name}. Nothing was sold."** The point does not have enough of this product. Remove it from the sale, or receive the goods first: see [Receive goods from a supplier](/en/warehouse/receive-goods).
- **"No connection to the server. Check the internet and try again."** Check the internet and press **Try again**.
- **"The server is busy. Try again in a moment."** Wait a moment and press **Try again**.
- **"If you are not sure it went through, tap the button again: the same sale is never recorded twice."** This appears when the answer was lost on the way. Press the button again. Partha will not record the sale a second time.
- **"Open a shift first."** or **"This shift is already closed."** Follow the link to the shift, open a new one and come back. See [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
- **"The currency does not match your open shift."** Sell in the currency of the shift, or close it and open a new one.
