A till that sold without internet sends its sales later. Partha always adds them, even when something about a sale looks wrong. Those sales are listed under **Problem sales**, so you can check each one. Nothing was lost.

::note
You need the access to handle problem sales. The owner of the business has it. Ask the owner if you do not see **Problem sales** in the menu.
::

## Check the problem sales

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Problem sales

Open your point, then **Cash desk** → **Problem sales**. You see the open problems, newest first: what happened, when, and on which till (**K1**, **K2**, …).

### Narrow the list

Press **Open**, **Resolved** or **All**. Under **Till** choose one till, and under **Problem** one kind of problem.

### Look at the sale

Press **Open the sale**. The receipt shows when it was sold, when Partha received it, the day it is counted on, the till, and its problems. A problem of the till itself, for example a wrong clock, has no sale to open.

### Mark it resolved

Press **Mark as resolved**. Under **Note**, write what you checked or did, for whoever looks later, then press **Resolve**. The problem moves to **Resolved**.
::

## The usual problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Sold more than in stock** | Check the stock of those products. Receive the goods you forgot to record, or count them: see [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count). |
| **Sold at a different price** | The till used an old price. The sale keeps that price. Check the prices of the point: see [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices). |
| **Arrived after the period was closed** | The sale is counted on the first open day. Nothing to do unless your accountant wants it otherwise. |
| **Sold before a stock count** | The count may already include this sale. Check the count of those products. |
| **Till clock is wrong** | The times of this till's sales may be off. Set the right time and time zone on the computer. |
| **Sales are on hold** | Partha stopped adding this till's sales at one sale. Call support: the later sales wait until it is repaired. |

Other problems explain themselves in the list. When you are not sure, write to support with the till code and the time.
