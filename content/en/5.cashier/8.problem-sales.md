A till that sold without internet sends its sales later. Partha always adds them, even when something about a sale looks wrong. Those sales are listed under **Problem sales**, so you can check each one. Nothing was lost.

::note
You need the access to handle problem sales. The owner of the business has it. Ask the owner if you do not see **Problem sales** in the menu.
::

## Check the problem sales

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Problem sales

Open your point, then **Cash desk** → **Problem sales**. You see the open problems, newest first: what happened, when, and on which till (**K1**, **K2**, …).

:::screenshot{src="cashier/problem-sales-1" alt="Problem sales: Open, Resolved and All, the Till and Problem filters, and two open problems"}
:::

### Narrow the list

Press **Open**, **Resolved** or **All**. Under **Till** choose one till, and under **Problem** one kind of problem.

### Look at the sale

Press **Open the sale**. The receipt shows **Sold**, **Received by Partha**, **Counted on**, **Till** and **Problems with this sale**. A problem of the till itself, for example a wrong clock, has no sale to open.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/problem-sales-2" alt="A receipt with Sold, Received by Partha, Counted on, Till and the problem with this sale"}
:::

### Mark it resolved

Press **Mark as resolved**. Under **Note** (optional), write what you checked or did, for whoever looks later, then press **Resolve**. The problem moves to **Resolved**.

:::screenshot{src="cashier/problem-sales-3" alt="A problem with a note typed in, and the Resolve button"}
:::

## Sales made for a closed day

When a past day is closed, its sales, profit and tax no longer change. A closed day can go up to yesterday: today is never closed.

A till that sold without internet may send a sale or a refund after its day was closed. A sale made on a point in another time zone can also fall on a closed day. Partha never refuses it. It counts the sale on the first open day, so the reports of the closed day do not change. The sale keeps its real time, so the receipt and the time of the sale stay true.

You see such a sale here, with **Arrived after the period was closed** and **Counted on {date}.** A sale made in the back office or in the browser has no till: the list shows a dash and **Web till / back office**. Open the sale to see both days: **Sold** shows the real time and **Counted on** the day it is counted on.

Nothing is lost and nothing needs fixing. Check the sale if your accountant needs it on the day it was made.

## The usual problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Sold more than in stock** | Check the stock of those products. Receive the goods you forgot to record, or count them: see [Count your stock](/en/warehouse/stock-count). |
| **Sold at a different price** | The till used a price that was not the price of that day. The sale keeps that price. Check the prices of the point: see [Set a price](/en/prices/set-prices). |
| **Arrived after the period was closed** | The sale is counted on the first open day: see [Sales made for a closed day](#closed-period). Nothing to do unless your accountant wants it otherwise. |
| **Sold before a stock count** | The count may already include this sale. Check the count of those products. |
| **Undo recorded in another shift** | A cash entry (**Cash in**, **Pay out** or **Cash drop**) was undone in a later shift than the one it was made in, for example when the till was offline and a new shift was opened before the undo was sent. If the first shift was still open, Partha put the undo in that shift. If it was already closed, the undo stays in the later shift, so the counted shift does not change. Look at **Should be in the drawer** of both shifts, and ask the cashier what happened to the cash. The row has the label **Cash in and out**. |
| **Cash in or out after the shift was closed** | A cash entry reached Partha after its shift was closed. It is added to that shift, and the reports say so under over or short: see [Sales and cash that arrived after close](/en/reports/cash-report#after-close). |
| **More cash taken than was in the drawer** | A pay out or cash drop was larger than the cash the drawer should have held at that moment. It still counts. Check the drawer and the count of the shift. |
| **Refund paid back too much cash** | The till gave back more cash for a refund than the sale had left in cash: the sale was paid by card, or part of its cash was already given back. The cash drawer may be short. Ask the cashier how the money was returned. |
| **Till clock is wrong** | The times of this till's sales may be off. Set the right time and time zone on the computer. |
| **Sales are on hold** | Partha stopped adding this till's sales at one sale. Call support: the later sales wait until it is repaired. |

A problem about cash in or out has no sale to open. It has the label **Cash in and out** instead. Other problems explain themselves in the list. When you are not sure, write to support with the till code and the time.
