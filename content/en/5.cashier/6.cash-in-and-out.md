Sometimes cash goes into the drawer or leaves it without a sale: you bring more change, pay a delivery driver, or take the money to the safe. Record it in Partha. Then the cash that should be in the drawer stays right, and at the close you see a real difference, not one you made yourself.

::note
You record cash at the till, in your own open :term[shift]{id="shift"}, and only while the till is online. Recording cash is part of the **Cashier** role. If you do not see the buttons, ask the owner of the business: see [Roles and what each can do](/en/team/roles-and-permissions#cash-desk).
::

## Three kinds of cash

These are the three kinds of :term[cash in and out]{id="cash-movement"}:

| Button | Use it when you | What it does to the drawer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cash in** | Put cash into the drawer, for example more change. | Adds the amount |
| **Pay out** | Pay for something with cash from the drawer. | Takes the amount out |
| **Cash drop** | Take cash out to keep it in the safe or take it to the bank. | Takes the amount out |

A sale and a refund are not recorded here: Partha counts them by itself.

## Record cash in or out

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the Shift screen

On the till, press **Shift** in the top bar. You see your open shift and, under it, **Cash in and out of the drawer** with three buttons: **Cash in**, **Pay out** and **Cash drop**.

<!-- SCREENSHOT TODO cashier/cash-in-and-out-1: the till's Shift screen of an open shift, the drawer card with a cash in line, and the section "Cash in and out of the drawer" with the three buttons. Needs a recipe in partha-js docs-shots; see the PR description. -->

### Press the button for what you do

Press **Cash in**, **Pay out** or **Cash drop**. A short form opens. At the top, **In the drawer now** shows how much cash the drawer should hold now.

### Type the amount

Type the amount in **Amount**. For a pay out or a cash drop, you cannot take out more than **In the drawer now**. If you type more, the form says so at once.

### Choose what it is for

Under **What for**, press the reason, for example **More change for the drawer** or **Small expense for the shop**. The list of reasons depends on the country of your business: see [Reasons in each country](#country-differences).

### Write a note if asked

The note field says **Note (optional)** or **Note (required)**. Write in a few words what the cash was for, for example "plastic bags from the market". A note is always required for the reason **Other**.

### Press the button at the bottom

Press **Put cash in**, **Pay out** or **Take cash out**. The form closes and the screen says, for example, **Cash in of 50.00 recorded.** The cash appears in the list under the buttons, and the drawer card shows it as its own line.

::tip
Paid a supplier in cash from the drawer? Record a **Pay out** with **Payment to a supplier** here, and also record the payment on the delivery, so Partha knows the debt is paid: see [Pay your suppliers](/en/warehouse/pay-suppliers). Partha does not link the two by itself.
::

## Reasons in each country

The reasons under **What for**, and when a note is required, depend on the country of your business.

::tabs
  :::tabs-item{label="Tajikistan"}
  - **Cash in**: **More change for the drawer**, **Change from the bank**, **Other**.
  - **Pay out**: **Small expense for the shop**, **Payment to a supplier**, **Wages or an advance**, **The owner takes cash**, **Other**. Partha asks for a note for **every** pay out.
  - **Cash drop**: **Into the safe**, **Taken to the bank**, **Handed to a cash collection service**, **Other**.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="United Kingdom"}
  - **Cash in**: **More change for the drawer**, **Change from the bank**, **Other**.
  - **Pay out**: **Small expense for the shop**, **Payment to a supplier**, **Wages or an advance**, **Tips paid to staff**, **The owner takes cash**, **Other**.
  - **Cash drop**: **Into the safe**, **Taken to the bank**, **Handed to a cash collection service**, **Other**.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Other countries"}
  - **Cash in**: **More change for the drawer**, **Change from the bank**, **Other**.
  - **Pay out**: **Small expense for the shop**, **Payment to a supplier**, **Wages or an advance**, **The owner takes cash**, **Other**.
  - **Cash drop**: **Into the safe**, **Taken to the bank**, **Handed to a cash collection service**, **Other**.
  :::

::note
These lists are what the app does today. They are not legal advice: check the cash rules of your country, for example what papers you must keep for cash paid out, with your accountant.

[]{#drawer-lines}

## How Partha counts the cash in the drawer

The Shift screen shows the cash of the drawer line by line:

- **Cash at the start**: what you typed when you opened the shift.
- **Cash sales**: the cash customers paid, minus the change you gave back.
- **Cash refunds**: the cash you gave back for refunds.
- One line for each cash in, pay out and cash drop, with its reason, for example **Cash in: More change for the drawer**.
- **Should be in the drawer**: what all the lines above add up to.

For example: you start with 200.00, sell for 350.00 in cash, refund 20.00, put in 50.00 of change, pay out 30.00 for bags and drop 300.00 into the safe. Should be in the drawer: 200.00 + 350.00 − 20.00 + 50.00 − 30.00 − 300.00 = 250.00.

When you close the shift, you count the drawer and Partha compares your count with this amount: see [Over and short](/en/cashier/close-shift#over-or-short).

[]{#undo}

## Undo an entry made by mistake

An entry is never changed or deleted. If you typed a wrong amount, you undo the entry, and then record the right amount again. The entry stays in the list, marked **undone**, and a new line appears that **undoes an earlier entry**. The cash that should be in the drawer goes back to what it was.

::tabs
  :::tabs-item{label="Till in the browser"}
  1. On the Shift screen, find the entry in the list under the buttons.
  2. Press **Undo** next to it.
  3. Partha asks **Undo this entry?** Press **Undo**.

  You see **Undo** only if the owner gave you the access **Undo** for **Cash in and out of the drawer**. It is not part of any role.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Windows till"}
  The Windows till has no **Undo** button yet. Ask the owner or a manager to undo the entry in the back office while your shift is still open: see [Undo cash entries](/en/reports/open-shifts#undo).
  :::
::

::warning
You can undo an entry only while its shift is open, and only once. After the shift is closed, the drawer has been counted, so a mistake shows as over or short in that shift. Write what happened in the note when you close.
::

[]{#troubleshooting}

## If something went wrong

### The buttons are off

When the till cannot record cash now, the buttons are grey and a message under them says why. Fix the cause, then press **Check again**.

- **"Cash in and out needs a connection to the server. The till is offline now: nothing is saved for later."** The till has no internet. Cash in and out is never saved for later, unlike sales on the Windows till. Keep the cash or the paper receipt aside, and record it as soon as the till is online again.
- **"You signed in while the till was offline. Lock the till and sign in again while it is online to record cash."** Lock the till, wait until it is online, and sign in again with your PIN.
- **"This shift has not reached the server yet. Wait a moment while the till sends it, then check again."** You opened the shift without internet. The till sends it by itself when it is online. Wait a moment, then press **Check again**.
- **"The server still has an older shift of yours on this till. Wait until the till has sent everything, then check again."** The till is still sending an earlier shift. Wait, then press **Check again**.
- **"This shift is already closed on the server (in the back office, or by the till itself). Count the drawer, close the shift here, then open a new one before you sell again."** A manager closed your shift in the back office. See [Your shift was closed in the back office](/en/cashier/close-shift#closed-in-back-office).
- **"Open a shift first."** Open a shift: see [Open a shift](/en/cashier/open-shift).
- **"The server did not answer. Check again in a moment."** Wait a little and press **Check again**.
- **"You are not allowed to record cash in or out. Ask the owner of the business."** You do not have the access **Record** for **Cash in and out of the drawer**. Ask the owner.
- **You do not see Cash in and out of the drawer at all.** You do not have the access **Record** for **Cash in and out of the drawer**. Ask the owner of the business.

### After you press the button

- **"The drawer holds only 120.00."** or **"The drawer does not have that much cash. It should hold 120.00."** You cannot take out more than should be in the drawer. Check the amount. If there really is more cash in the drawer, a sale or a cash in may be missing: tell the owner.
- **"No connection to the server, so the cash is not recorded yet. Tap again when the till is back online: it will not be counted twice. Nothing is saved for later."** The connection dropped while the till was sending. Leave the form as it is. When the till is online, press the same button again. If the first try did reach the server, Partha records the cash only once.
- **"Cash in of 50.00 was already recorded when the connection dropped. It is recorded once."** The first try did reach the server. Nothing more to do.
- **"The 50.00 you sent while the connection was down did reach the server: it is in the list. Record it again only if this is more cash."** You see this when you open the form again. Check the list: the cash is there once. Record it again only if you really moved more cash.
- **"Cash in of 50.00 was recorded, but in another open shift of yours on the server. Tell the owner, and do not record it again."** The cash is recorded, but in another shift of yours. Do not record it again. Tell the owner, who can see both shifts in the back office.
- **"This day is in a closed period, so cash cannot be moved now. Ask the owner."** or **"The books are closed up to 31.10.2026, so cash cannot be moved now. Ask the owner."** The owner has closed the books up to that day, so no new cash can be recorded on it. Ask the owner.
- **"Write what the cash was for."** This reason needs a note. Write one.
- **"Choose what it is for."** Press a reason under **What for**.
- **"Enter an amount above zero."** Type the amount.
- **"The note can be at most 300 characters."** Make the note shorter.
- **"Check the amount and the note and try again."** or **"Something went wrong. Try again."** Check what you typed and press the button again.
- **"This reason cannot be used here. Choose another one."** Choose another reason from the list.
- **"This form was already sent with other figures. Look at the list before you try again."** Close the form and check the list. If the cash is not there, open the form again and record it.
- **"You are not allowed to do this. Ask the owner of the business."** You do not have the access for this. Ask the owner.
- **"The currency does not match the shift."** Close the shift and open a new one in the currency you use now.
- **"There is no open shift to put this into. Open a shift first."** or **"This shift is already closed."** Open a new shift, then record the cash.

### When you undo an entry

- **"Someone already undid this. Look at the list again."** Another person undid it first. Nothing more to do.
- **"This line already undoes another one and cannot be undone. Record the cash again instead."** To bring the cash back, record it again as a new entry.
- **"This cash entry no longer exists. Look at the list again."** The list has changed. Look at it again.
- **"The drawer does not have that much cash. It should hold 20.00."** Undoing a cash in takes that cash out of the drawer, and the drawer does not have it now.
